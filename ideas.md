# Pull start
- chainsaw
- end game

# Dad All Powerful
- end game DM ability

# reality zone
- allows players to move around
- dm can move into reality zone
- tiles slowly and randomly change to their 'reality' art (paper instead of sparkles or blood, grey/monochrome instead of colors)
- allows buildings to be built
- skeletons cannot exist in reality zone

# fantasy zone
- players cannot move into fantasy zone
- tiles slowly and randomly change to their 'fantasy' art (sparkles, vibrant colors, etc)
- does not allow buildings to be built
- skeletons

# Paper Pushers
- primary weapon is a staple gun
- melee weapon: huge pen or pencil
- harvest wood from trees to feed into paper factory to produce paper
- harvest iron from mines to build buildings
- smoke from smoke factories accumulates to power buildings
- smoke factories increase reality level
- paper factories increase reality level more than smoke factories
- paper can be used to create forms
- forms can be filled out to increase reality level

# monsters
- goblins attack factories and destroy them
- knightlings can randomly hit players and kill them
- gremlins pick up and move around resources 

# tax forms
- filled out tax forms must be brought to the IRS building to be filed
- filled out tax forms increase reality level a lot (more than producing paper does)

# Ammo
- Office max to restock on staples
- green mt dew cans act as 'mana' for dungeon master
  - casting spells and summoning monsters cost mana
  - if no mana the spell cannot be cast
  - picking up a green mt dew can increases mana

# Dungeon
- DM starts in a Dungeon
- explores dungeon and Levels up before finding the exit
- finds power ups
  - mt dew green
    - unlocks knightling
  - dice
- increase fantasy level
- baja blast boss
  - unlocks bemidji blizzard spell that slows down factories and player movement
- code red dragon
  - unlocks fireball
- some sort of tube
  - can be used like horadric cube in d2
  - combine all 3 cans of mt dew to unlock 'dad all powerful' ability
- can cozy
  - allows you to wield cold cans (baja blast)
- after exiting the dungeon they can interact and fight more directly with the paper pushers
- this gives time for the paper pushers to build up
