# US-024: Bounded map, layout, and cliff edge

**Status**: Done  
**Source**: new — map bounds / overworld fill  
**Priority**: P1  
**Roles**: Paper Pusher, Dungeon Master

## User Story

As either role, I play inside a finite map ringed by cliff edges so I cannot walk off the world, the dungeon sits on the right edge, Paper Pushers start on the left, and the rest of the interior is random overworld grass, dirt, and trees.

## Context

There is no match-wide play AABB today: zones are free-floating circles, dungeon generation uses its own `bounds_size` (default 24×24 cells), and the overworld is not filled with outside tiles.

This story owns the **map interior**, **cliff boundary**, **role layout** (dungeon east, Paper Pushers west), and **default overworld fill**. Tile catalogs are US-023 (outside) and the dungeon catalog. Occupancy is US-001 / US-003. Dungeon crawl/exit is US-015. Trees as harvestables are US-006.

## Independent Test

Start a match. The playable ground is a rectangle of outside grass/dirt with random trees, large enough that the generated dungeon AABB would fit in it four times. A cliff ring surrounds it; Paper Pusher and DM both stop at the cliff. The dungeon is committed against the **right** interior edge. Paper Pushers spawn on the **left** interior edge. Reality and Fantasy home rectangles lie entirely inside the interior. Walking off the cliff is impossible.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Given** a match has started, **When** a Paper Pusher or Dungeon Master tries to move past the map interior, **Then** they cannot occupy space beyond the cliff (they stop on the last walkable interior cell).
2. **Given** any other moving actor (monster, projectile that should collide with world), **When** it would leave the interior, **Then** it is blocked, destroyed, or clamped by a documented rule; it MUST NOT roam in the void. Player types are always blocked.
3. **Given** the generated dungeon AABB, **When** the map interior is measured, **Then** interior area is at least **4×** that dungeon AABB area (walkable cells inside the cliffs, excluding the cliff ring itself).
4. **Given** dungeon commit, **When** its placement is evaluated, **Then** the dungeon sits flush to the **right (east)** interior edge; it does not float in the map center and it does not overlap the cliff cells.
5. **Given** match start, **When** Paper Pushers spawn, **Then** they appear on the **left (west)** interior edge, on outside tiles, not in the dungeon and not on cliff cells.
6. **Given** the DM at match start, **When** they spawn, **Then** they appear at the dungeon entrance (US-015) inside that right-edge dungeon.
7. **Given** Reality and Fantasy home regions (US-001, US-003), **When** they are placed or grown, **Then** they remain entirely inside the map interior; they never extend past the cliffs.
8. **Given** a cell in the map interior that is not dungeon and not cliff, **When** the map is filled, **Then** it is an **outside** tile (US-023) whose ground kind is randomly grass or dirt (Neutral presentation until a zone claims it).
9. **Given** that overworld fill, **When** trees are scattered, **Then** random trees (existing `TreeDoodad` varieties) appear on a subset of interior outside cells, not on dungeon cells, not on cliffs, and not on the Paper Pusher spawn strip or dungeon exit adjacency.
10. **Given** a late-joining client, **When** they receive the map, **Then** they see the same interior size, cliff ring, dungeon placement, outside fill, and tree set.

## Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: The match MUST define a single axis-aligned **map interior** in tile cells, surrounded by a **cliff ring**. The interior is the only walkable play area for both player types.
- **FR-002**: Interior area (cell count) MUST be ≥ **4 ×** the generated dungeon AABB area. Suggested default for a dungeon AABB of `Wd × Hd` cells: interior `2·Wd × 2·Hd` (exactly 4× area), with the dungeon AABB placed flush to the east interior edge and vertically centered (or flush north if height equals `Hd`).
- **FR-003**: Neither Paper Pushers nor the Dungeon Master MUST be able to path, dash, knockback, or spawn beyond the interior. Server physics MUST clamp or reject movement that would leave it.
- **FR-004**: The generated dungeon MUST be committed against the **right** interior boundary. Dungeon cells MUST be fully inside the interior and MUST NOT replace cliff cells.
- **FR-005**: Paper Pusher spawn points MUST lie on the **left** interior boundary, on outside tiles, with enough clear cells for the Reality spawn set (US-001).
- **FR-006**: Reality home and Fantasy home MUST be created inside the interior. Zone growth and pockets MUST be **clipped** to the interior; a pocket that would extend past a cliff is truncated, not allowed to punch through.
- **FR-007**: Every interior cell that is not a dungeon cell MUST be filled with an outside grass or dirt tile (US-023), variety chosen randomly per cell (or per coherent patch), Neutral until zone drift (US-002 / US-004).
- **FR-008**: The server MUST scatter trees on interior outside cells with a configurable density (suggested 4–12% of eligible cells). Trees MUST use existing tree frames (`sprites/tree.png`). Eligible cells exclude: dungeon, cliffs, Paper Pusher spawn strip, dungeon exit neighbor cells, and building-blocked cells if any exist at fill time.
- **FR-009**: Cliff cells MUST use the **cliff edge** catalog (this story), not dungeon walls and not outside grass. They MUST have collision that blocks all player types.
- **FR-010**: Map interior, cliff placement, dungeon world origin, outside fill, and tree set MUST be host-authoritative and replicated, including to late joiners.

## Multiplayer Requirements

- **MR-001**: All peers MUST observe the same map interior rectangle, cliff ring, dungeon origin, tile fill, and tree positions.
- **MR-002**: Movement past the cliff MUST be rejected on the server even if a client predicts through it.

## Edge Cases

- Knockback / blizzard displacement / Fantasy push (US-003) would send a player off the map: clamp to the nearest interior walkable cell; do not kill them for leaving the map.
- Dungeon AABB taller than the planned interior height: grow interior height to fit the dungeon plus at least one cell of padding from the north/south cliffs, then still satisfy the 4× area rule (widen if needed).
- Projectiles fired at a cliff: collide and despawn; they MUST NOT continue into the void.
- Gremlin carrying loot to a cliff: it cannot leave the interior; it drops inside.
- Reality/Fantasy homes overlapping: still inside the same interior; occupancy rules unchanged (US-001 / US-003).
- Tree scatter landing on a future building footprint: buildings still require a clear area (existing placement check).

## Key Entities

- **Map Interior**: Axis-aligned walkable rectangle in tile cells, inside the cliff ring.
- **Cliff Ring**: Non-walkable boundary tiles around the interior.
- **Dungeon AABB**: Axis-aligned cell rect of the committed dungeon, flush to the east interior edge.
- **West Spawn Strip**: Outside cells along the west interior edge used for Paper Pusher spawns.

## Assumptions

- Cell size is 128×128 world units, matching dungeon floors.
- Default dungeon generation `bounds_size` is 24×24 unless the generator knobs change; a 4× map is then at least 48×48 interior cells in the 2×2 default, or equivalent area.
- “Right” / “left” are east / west in world space (+X is right).
- Random fill is Neutral grass/dirt; zone claim later restyles those same cells (US-002 / US-004).
- Existing tree sprites are sufficient for scatter (US-006 harvest behavior is separate).

## Out of Scope

- Outside grass/dirt variety art (US-023).
- Zone occupancy and pockets (US-001, US-003).
- Dungeon interior generation (existing generator + US-015).
- Harvest rules for trees (US-006).
- Mines (US-007), though they must also sit in the interior overworld.

## Required New Art Assets

Cliffs are **128×128** cells, same offset as `level/floor.tscn` / `level/wall.tscn` (`(0, -63)`), south-foot y-sort, pixel 3/4. They should read as a **drop-off** (dirt/rock/grass lip), not as cubicle-stone dungeon walls. Grass on the interior side of a cliff must meet Neutral outside tiles.

For a rectangular map, the minimum set is four edges and four outer corners (same idea as `sprites/cubicle_stone_wall.png` frames, but a new catalog).

| Asset | Dimensions | Match | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cliff edge N | 128×128 | `sprites/cubicle_stone_wall.png` cell size and offset | North lip; interior is south of this tile. |
| Cliff edge E | 128×128 | Same | East lip (right map edge, beside the dungeon). |
| Cliff edge S | 128×128 | Same; south **face** like dungeon walls | South drop-off so approaching from the south y-sorts correctly. |
| Cliff edge W | 128×128 | Same | West lip (Paper Pusher side). |
| Cliff corner NW, NE, SW, SE | 128×128 each | Same | Outer corners of the ring. |
| Cliff void / beyond (optional) | 128×128 or empty | — | Visual past the ring; never walkable. Solid color/empty is acceptable. |

Do **not** reuse `cubicle_stone_wall.png` as the world border. Trees for scatter already exist (`sprites/tree.png`, 32×32 frames).
