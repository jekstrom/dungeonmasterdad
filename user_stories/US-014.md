# US-014: Mountain Dew cans as Dungeon Master mana

**Status**: Draft  
**Source**: `ideas.md` — Ammo  
**Priority**: P1  
**Roles**: Dungeon Master

## User Story

As the Dungeon Master, I spend Mountain Dew mana to cast spells and summon monsters, and I cannot cast if I have none, so that my power is a resource I have to pick up and manage.

## Context

Today summons spend `DmManager.fantasy_level` (gremlin/knight buttons subtract 150) and fireball is gated by `DmUnlocks` without a spendable mana pool. Green Mt Dew already exists as a pickup (`pickups/mtdew/`) and is intended both as mana (this story) and as the knightling unlock (US-016).

Fantasy Level remains the **zone expansion / progression** value. Mana is a **separate spend resource**.

## Independent Test

Start the DM at 0 mana: fireball, gremlin, knight, blizzard all refuse. Pick up a green Mt Dew can: mana increases. Cast a spell: mana decreases by its cost and the spell happens. Try again at 0: refuse.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Given** the DM's mana is 0, **When** they attempt to cast a spell or summon a monster, **Then** the action does not occur and mana stays 0.
2. **Given** the DM's mana is greater than 0 but less than a spell's cost, **When** they attempt that spell, **Then** it does not occur and mana is unchanged.
3. **Given** the DM has at least the required mana, **When** they successfully cast or summon, **Then** mana decreases by that ability's cost and the ability executes once.
4. **Given** a green Mt Dew can pickup, **When** the DM picks it up, **Then** mana increases by the configured amount, not past max mana.
5. **Given** a Paper Pusher walks over a green Mt Dew can, **When** pickup is resolved, **Then** they do not gain DM mana (pickup remains DM-only, matching `ItemData.pickup_char`).
6. **Given** mana changes, **When** the DM HUD is shown, **Then** it displays current and max mana.
7. **Given** Fantasy Level changes, **When** a cast happens, **Then** mana is spent rather than Fantasy Level, unless a specific ability also lists a Fantasy Level effect.

## Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: The DM MUST have a host-authoritative mana pool with current and max values (suggested max 100).
- **FR-002**: Picking up a green Mt Dew can MUST add mana (suggested +25) up to max.
- **FR-003**: Every spell and summon MUST declare an integer mana cost. Suggested defaults: gremlin 20, knightling 40, fireball 15, Bemidji Blizzard 30, Dad All Powerful uses US-021's rule (may be 0 if it is a one-shot unlock ability).
- **FR-004**: If current mana < cost, the server MUST reject the cast with no side effects.
- **FR-005**: A successful cast MUST deduct cost atomically on the server before the ability's world effect.
- **FR-006**: Fantasy Level MUST NOT be deducted as the cast currency (it may still change from pickups, bosses, and dice in US-016–US-018).
- **FR-007**: Mana MUST replicate to the DM client HUD; other clients may see a simple indicator but must not author the value.
- **FR-008**: Unlock gates (fireball, knightling, blizzard, Dad All Powerful) MUST still apply: mana is necessary but not sufficient.

## Multiplayer Requirements

- **MR-001**: Cast validation and mana deduction MUST run on the host/server.
- **MR-002**: Clients MUST NOT be able to cast by faking HUD button presses without server mana.

## Edge Cases

- Two HUD buttons pressed in one frame: process sequentially; second fails if mana is now insufficient.
- Dew pickup at max mana: can is still consumed or left on the ground? Consume and waste overflow (no drop) unless the pickup is `auto_use` false — consume, clamp mana, do not duplicate cans.
- Fireball currently *adds* Fantasy Level on the HUD; that grant is out of scope here and should not also ignore mana.

## Key Entities

- **Mana**: DM cast resource, restored by green Mt Dew.
- **Green Mt Dew Can**: World pickup that restores mana (and unlocks knightling in US-016).

## Assumptions

- Starting mana at match start is 0 or a small stipend (suggested 0) so the dungeon crawl (US-015) is how the DM first powers up.
- Baja Blast and Code Red are not generic mana cans; they are boss unlocks (US-017, US-018) unless later tuned as flavor-specific Dew.

## Out of Scope

- Knightling unlock on first green Dew (US-016).
- Spell effect bodies (fireball exists; blizzard is US-017).
- Combining cans in the cube (US-019).

## Required New Art Assets

None required. Reuse `pickups/mtdew/mtdew.png` (**32×32**) for the world can and as the mana HUD pip. A ColorRect/bar is enough for the meter; do not invent a second dew can silhouette.
