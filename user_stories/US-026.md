# US-026: Ambient zone particles

**Status**: Draft  
**Source**: James — ambient zone particles  
**Priority**: P2  
**Roles**: Paper Pusher, Dungeon Master

## User Story

As a player in the match, I see **subtle, infrequent** dust in Reality-claimed ground and sparkles in Fantasy-claimed ground so each zone reads in the air, not only on the tile wash.

## Context

Overlays are US-001 / US-003. Tile art drift is US-002 / US-004. Convert puffs (`reality_drift_puff.png`, `fantasy_drift_puff.png`) fire **once on a tile convert** and stay those stories.

This story is **standing ambience**: local VFX that play while a cell is claimed. It does not change occupancy, claim, collision, or walkability. It does not replicate particle emitting (claim is already replicated; each client plays its own dust/sparkles).

Claim is the live Reality / Fantasy claim (home ∪ pockets, pockets override, homes exclusive per US-025). Unclaimed cells are quiet.

## Independent Test

Stand in Reality home: occasional grey/paper dust, not a convert puff, not every frame. Walk into Fantasy home (or a Fantasy pocket): dust stops, infrequent sparkles play. A second window does **not** need to match burst timing. Occupancy still works (PP walk, DM walk, buildings, skeletons).

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Given** a cell whose center is Reality-claimed, **When** a player looks at it for a few seconds, **Then** they may see subtle infrequent dust; they do not see Fantasy sparkles from this story.
2. **Given** a cell whose center is Fantasy-claimed, **When** a player looks at it for a few seconds, **Then** they may see subtle infrequent sparkles; they do not see Reality dust from this story.
3. **Given** a cell that is not Reality-claimed and not Fantasy-claimed, **When** time passes, **Then** this story emits neither dust nor sparkles there.
4. **Given** a tile convert puff (US-002 / US-004), **When** ambience is playing, **Then** the puff is a separate one-shot; ambience is not that puff looped.
5. **Given** two peers in the same claimed cell, **When** ambience plays, **Then** emitting is local (burst times need not match). Claim overlays and occupancy still match.
6. **Given** many claimed cells, **When** ambience is running, **Then** the server/client does not scan every map cell every physics frame.

## Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: While a cell is **Reality-claimed**, clients MAY emit subtle, infrequent dust VFX on or above that cell. Density MUST stay low (suggested: seconds between pops per nearby cell, not a fog).
- **FR-002**: While a cell is **Fantasy-claimed**, clients MAY emit subtle, infrequent sparkle VFX on or above that cell. Same density bar as FR-001.
- **FR-003**: Ambience MUST follow live claim (home ∪ pockets). Pocket override and US-025 exclusive homes apply. Unclaimed cells MUST be quiet.
- **FR-004**: VFX MUST be local only. Do **not** replicate particle emitting, RNG seeds, or burst times. Use already-replicated claim.
- **FR-005**: Scheduling MUST be claim-change and/or visible-set driven. Do **not** scan every map / outside tile every physics frame.
- **FR-006**: This story MUST NOT change occupancy, claim, collision, walkability, y-sort of actors, or tile kind. Missing VFX MUST NOT fail claim or drift.
- **FR-007**: Convert puffs remain US-002 / US-004. Do not reuse those strips as the looping ambience, and do not block convert puffs on this story.
- **FR-008**: This story MUST NOT implement blizzard slow (US-017) or game over.

## Multiplayer Requirements

- **MR-001**: Particle emitting MUST NOT be a replicated property.
- **MR-002**: Peers MUST still share the same claim (existing zone replication). Ambience is each client's read of that claim.

## Edge Cases

- Pocket appears/expires: ambience type follows the new claim without a full-map scan.
- Home shrinks (US-025): cells that leave a claim go quiet immediately.
- Cell is both dungeon catalog and Fantasy-claimed: sparkles are allowed; do not restyle the floor (US-023 / US-004).
- Camera far from a claimed cell: prefer spawning only for a visible / nearby set.
- Convert puff and ambience on the same cell: both may play; they are different VFX.

## Key Entities

- **Ambient dust**: Local Reality-claim VFX. Not occupancy. Not a convert puff.
- **Ambient sparkle**: Local Fantasy-claim VFX. Not occupancy. Not a convert puff.

## Assumptions

- `sprites/sparks.png` may be reused for Fantasy sparkles if it reads at this scale; otherwise a small new strip. Reality dust may be a small new strip or a CPU/GPU particle with grey/paper color. Convert puff sheets stay one-shots.
- Infrequent means noticeable over several seconds, not a per-frame blizzard of motes.

## Out of Scope

- Convert puffs (US-002, US-004).
- Tile art drift and overlays.
- Occupancy, buildings, skeletons, PP walk (US-001, US-003, US-003 T011).
- Blizzard slow (US-017).
- Game over when one zone covers the map.
- Catalog membership (US-023).

## Required New Art Assets

Optional if existing `sprites/sparks.png` / a tinted particle already reads. Convert puff sheets are **not** these assets.

| Asset | Dimensions | Match | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reality ambient dust | small (≤32×32 frames) | grey paper motes; quieter than `reality_drift_puff.png` | Looping/idle motes, not a convert burst. |
| Fantasy ambient sparkle | small (≤32×32 frames) | sparkle language like `sprites/sparks.png` | Infrequent twinkles, not `fantasy_drift_puff.png`. |
