# US-010: Restock staples at Office Max

**Status**: Draft  
**Source**: `ideas.md` — Ammo  
**Priority**: P2  
**Roles**: Paper Pusher

## User Story

As a Paper Pusher, I visit Office Max to restock staples so that my staple gun (US-005) has a map-tied ammo supply instead of infinite fire.

## Context

US-005 introduces a per-player staple magazine. This story is the only intended full restock. There is no Office Max building yet.

## Independent Test

Empty the magazine, walk to an enabled Office Max, restock, confirm magazine is full. Attempt restock away from Office Max and confirm it fails. A second Office Max placement is rejected.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Given** a Paper Pusher is in interact range of an enabled Office Max and their magazine is not full, **When** they restock, **Then** their staple magazine is set to max.
2. **Given** the magazine is already full, **When** they restock, **Then** nothing changes (no error required beyond a no-op or "already full" feedback).
3. **Given** the player is not in range of Office Max, **When** they press restock / interact, **Then** the magazine does not change.
4. **Given** Office Max has not been built, **When** a player needs ammo, **Then** there is no other full-restock source from this story.
5. **Given** an Office Max already exists, **When** another Office Max placement is requested, **Then** the server rejects it.
6. **Given** Office Max is destroyed (US-011), **When** players try to restock, **Then** restock is unavailable until it is rebuilt.

## Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: Office Max MUST be a unique buildable (max one enabled per match), placeable under US-001 / US-003 rules, costing iron (US-007).
- **FR-002**: Interacting with Office Max MUST refill the interacting player's staple magazine to its maximum (US-005).
- **FR-003**: Restock MUST be instant or a short channel (suggested default ≤1s) and MUST NOT consume paper, wood, iron, or smoke.
- **FR-004**: Restock MUST be host-authoritative per player magazine.
- **FR-005**: Office Max MUST NOT refill other players' magazines without their own interact.

## Multiplayer Requirements

- **MR-001**: Two players may restock in sequence; each magazine is independent.
- **MR-002**: Uniqueness of Office Max MUST be enforced on the server.

## Edge Cases

- Player interacts while dying: if the interact completes before death, magazine is full on respawn; if not, magazine stays empty.
- Building ghost/preview is not an enabled Office Max.

## Key Entities

- **Office Max**: Unique restock building.
- **Staple Magazine**: Per-player ammo from US-005.

## Assumptions

- Partial refill is not needed; interact always fills to max.
- There is no staple item in inventory; ammo lives on the weapon.

## Out of Scope

- Staple gun firing (US-005).
- IRS (US-009).
- DM mana (US-014).
