# US-016: Dungeon power-ups — green Dew and dice

**Status**: Headless complete; play pass not run  
**Source**: `ideas.md` — Dungeon  
**Priority**: P2  
**Roles**: Dungeon Master

## User Story

As the Dungeon Master exploring the dungeon, I find green Mountain Dew and dice so that I gain mana, unlock knightlings, and raise Fantasy Level before I exit.

## Context

Pickups already include Mt Dew, d6, and d20, with `ItemData` effects. Code Red already unlocks fireball and adds Fantasy Level (`ItemEffectUnlockFireball`). Green Dew does not yet unlock knightling. Dice do not have a documented Fantasy Level effect in ideas.md beyond "dice" listed under power-ups that "increase fantasy level."

Knightling combat is US-012. Mana from green Dew is US-014. This story owns **placement in the dungeon** and the **knightling unlock** plus **dice → Fantasy Level**.

## Independent Test

Run a dungeon: find a green Dew can as the DM, pick it up, mana rises (US-014) and knightling summon becomes available on the HUD. Find a die, pick it up, Fantasy Level increases and the Fantasy home rectangle grows. Paper Pushers cannot use these pickups.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Given** a generated dungeon, **When** generation completes, **Then** it contains at least one green Mt Dew pickup and at least one die pickup (d6 and/or d20) in reachable cells that are not the entrance or exit.
2. **Given** the DM picks up green Mt Dew for the first time in the match, **When** the pickup resolves, **Then** knightling summon is unlocked in addition to the mana grant from US-014.
3. **Given** knightling is already unlocked, **When** another green Dew is picked up, **Then** mana is granted and the unlock stays true (no lock/toggle).
4. **Given** the DM HUD after the unlock, **When** they have enough mana (US-014), **Then** the spawn-knightling action is usable.
5. **Given** the DM picks up a d6, **When** the pickup resolves, **Then** Fantasy Level increases by a d6-scale amount.
6. **Given** the DM picks up a d20, **When** the pickup resolves, **Then** Fantasy Level increases by a larger d20-scale amount.
7. **Given** a Paper Pusher overlaps a DM-only dungeon pickup, **When** pickup is evaluated, **Then** they do not collect it.

## Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: Dungeon generation MUST place green Mt Dew and dice from existing pickup resources onto reachable floor cells, never on entrance/exit cells.
- **FR-002**: First green Dew of the match MUST call the unlock path for knightling (`DmUnlocks` or equivalent) on the server.
- **FR-003**: Dice MUST increase Fantasy Level on use/pickup (suggested defaults: d6 +6, d20 +20, or a roll 1–N added).
- **FR-004**: Fantasy Level increases MUST grow the Fantasy **home rectangle** (US-003), not a circle.
- **FR-005**: These pickups MUST be `dm_only`.
- **FR-006**: Pickup effects MUST be host-authoritative and replicated (unlock + HUD).

## Multiplayer Requirements

- **MR-001**: Only one peer (the DM) receives the unlock; all peers may see monster-summon availability indirectly when a knightling appears.
- **MR-002**: Pickup consumption MUST happen once; no double-collect across peers.

## Edge Cases

- Two dice stacked in one cell: both collectable sequentially.
- DM at whatever Fantasy cap exists: still consume the die; clamp level if a cap is added later.
- Knight spawn button currently subtracts Fantasy Level: after US-014 it must spend mana instead; this story must not reintroduce a Fantasy Level tax on the unlock itself.

## Key Entities

- **Green Mt Dew Can**: Mana (US-014) + first-pick knightling unlock.
- **d6 / d20**: Fantasy Level power-ups.
- **Knightling Unlock**: Persistent DM ability flag.

## Assumptions

- Dice "increase fantasy level" is the entire die effect; they do not also restore mana.
- Quantity per dungeon is tunable; minimum is one Dew and one die so the story is testable.

## Out of Scope

- Baja Blast boss (US-017), Code Red dragon (US-018).
- Knightling lethal combat (US-012).
- Cube combining cans (US-019).

## Required New Art Assets

None required. Reuse `pickups/mtdew/mtdew.png`, `pickups/d6/d6.png`, and `pickups/d20/d20.png` (all **32×32**). Knightling HUD reuses `monsters/knight/knight.png`.
