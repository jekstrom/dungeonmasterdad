# US-027: Baja Blast Carbonated Jet

**Status**: Draft  
**Source**: James — 3 Baja Blast specials (implement first)  
**Priority**: P2  
**Roles**: Dungeon Master

## User Story

As the Dungeon Master fighting the Baja Blast boss, I see a readable arm-point telegraph and then a piercing high-velocity stream of neon Baja syrup so I can dodge a skill shot that is not the US-028 fountain slick.

## Context

Builds on US-017 boss combat (`monsters/baja_boss.gd`, blast state). US-017 **blast** is a short Baja spit/hurtbox. **Carbonated Jet** is a telegraphed **linear piercing skill shot**. US-028 is the **fountain slick** environmental (not a boss move). Sugar Rush is US-029. Primary target is the **DM in the dungeon**. Paper Pusher occupancy is unchanged (US-003 T011).

## Independent Test

Aggro the Baja Blast boss. It points an arm and shows a linear telegraph, then fires a neon syrup stream that punches through and can hit the DM. A second window sees the same telegraph and stream. The US-028 fountain slick (if present) is a different system.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Given** the boss chooses Carbonated Jet, **When** the windup starts, **Then** a linear telegraph is readable before the stream fires.
2. **Given** the stream fires, **When** it travels, **Then** it is a piercing high-velocity neon Baja syrup shot along that line, host-authoritative.
3. **Given** the stream overlaps the DM, **When** the host resolves, **Then** the DM is hit (dungeon combat).
4. **Given** the US-028 fountain splash or US-017 blast, **When** Jet plays, **Then** it is a distinct move (not those VFX, not that hit shape).

## Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: Carbonated Jet MUST be a telegraphed linear skill shot. The boss points an arm (or equivalent facing pose) and the lane MUST be readable before fire.
- **FR-002**: The shot MUST be a piercing high-velocity stream of neon Baja syrup along the telegraphed line. It MUST NOT stop on the first target.
- **FR-003**: Hit detection and damage MUST be host-authoritative. The stream MUST be able to hit the DM in the dungeon.
- **FR-004**: Jet MUST be distinct from the US-028 fountain splash/slick and from US-017 blast.
- **FR-005**: Telegraph, stream spawn, and hits MUST replicate to peers. Clients may predict VFX.

## Multiplayer Requirements

- **MR-001**: Telegraph start, stream, and hits MUST be server-authored.
- **MR-002**: Peers MUST see the same lane and the same hit outcome.

## Edge Cases

- DM behind another damageable: stream continues (piercing).
- Boss dies during telegraph: cancel; do not fire.
- Paper Pusher in the dungeon lane: may be hit; occupancy T011 is unchanged (no wall, no push-out).

## Key Entities

- **Carbonated Jet**: Telegraphed piercing syrup stream.
- **Telegraph**: Readable linear lane before fire.

## Assumptions

- Implement this special first among US-027–029.
- Suggested tell: 0.4–0.8s. Suggested stream is thin and fast, not a wave.

## Out of Scope

- Fountain slick (US-028), Sugar Rush (US-029).
- US-019 cube, US-020 cozy, US-018 fireball.
- Bemidji Blizzard pocket/slow (US-017 T005).
- PP occupancy (US-003 T011).
