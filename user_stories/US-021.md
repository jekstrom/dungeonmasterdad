# US-021: Dad All Powerful endgame ability

**Status**: Draft  
**Source**: `ideas.md` — Dad All Powerful, Dungeon  
**Priority**: P3  
**Roles**: Dungeon Master, Paper Pusher

## User Story

As the Dungeon Master, after I combine all three Dew cans in the cube tube, I can activate Dad All Powerful so that the match has a true endgame DM form for fighting the built-up office.

## Context

Unlock path is US-019. Ideas.md only says this is an "end game DM ability." The paired Paper Pusher endgame is the pull-start chainsaw (US-022). After dungeon exit (US-015) the DM already fights directly; this is the escalation past that.

## Independent Test

Unlock via the cube. Activate once with whatever cost the story defines. For the duration: the DM is visibly transformed, can smash buildings, ignore remaining office safety, and cast unlocked spells without going dry on mana. When it ends, they return to normal DM rules. A second activation in the same match is refused.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Given** Dad All Powerful is locked, **When** the DM presses the ability, **Then** nothing happens.
2. **Given** it is unlocked and not yet used this match, **When** the DM activates it, **Then** they enter the Dad All Powerful form for a configurable duration (suggested 20s).
3. **Given** the form is active, **When** the DM melees a Paper Pusher, **Then** hits apply enhanced damage (suggested 2× DM melee).
4. **Given** the form is active, **When** the DM melees an enabled building, **Then** the building takes heavy damage (suggested enough to destroy a smoke factory in 2–3 hits).
5. **Given** the form is active, **When** the DM casts an already-unlocked spell or summon, **Then** mana is not required (casts still respect unlock gates).
6. **Given** the form is active, **When** zone rules are evaluated, **Then** the DM still cannot be blocked from Reality (already true in US-001) and Paper Pushers still cannot enter Fantasy (US-003 remains).
7. **Given** the duration ends, **When** the next action runs, **Then** enhanced melee, building smash, and free casts end; remaining mana is unchanged (not emptied, not refilled).
8. **Given** the form has already been used this match, **When** the DM activates again, **Then** it is refused.
9. **Given** peers are watching, **When** the form starts and ends, **Then** they see the same transformation window and damage outcomes.

## Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: Dad All Powerful MUST be a persistent unlock flag set only by US-019's recipe (or a debug cheat).
- **FR-002**: Activation MUST be once per match, host-authoritative, with a visible start and end.
- **FR-003**: While active, the DM MUST deal increased melee damage to Paper Pushers and MUST be able to damage buildings.
- **FR-004**: While active, unlocked spells/summons MUST skip mana cost (US-014 still applies when the form is down).
- **FR-005**: The form MUST NOT automatically win the match; Paper Pushers can still kill the DM if they survive the window (chainsaw is US-022).
- **FR-006**: Death during the form ends the form immediately; the once-per-match use is still consumed.
- **FR-007**: HUD MUST show unlock state, whether it has been used, and remaining duration while active.

## Multiplayer Requirements

- **MR-001**: Start, end, damage, and free-cast validation MUST run on the server.
- **MR-002**: All peers MUST agree on whether the form is active.

## Edge Cases

- Activating at 0 mana: allowed; that is the point.
- Activating inside the dungeon before exit: allowed but likely a waste; do not hard-block.
- Blizzard already slowing factories: form does not cancel it.

## Key Entities

- **Dad All Powerful**: Once-per-match DM transformation.
- **Unlock Flag**: Set by the cube recipe.

## Assumptions

- Visual: oversized, louder, Mountain-Dew-charged dad — exact art is not this story.
- Suggested duration 20s is a first tuning value.

## Out of Scope

- Cube crafting steps (US-019).
- Pull-start chainsaw (US-022).
- Match win/lose conditions (not in ideas.md).
