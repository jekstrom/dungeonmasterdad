# US-020: Can cozy lets the DM wield cold cans

**Status**: Draft  
**Source**: `ideas.md` — Dungeon  
**Priority**: P3  
**Roles**: Dungeon Master

## User Story

As the Dungeon Master, I find a can cozy so that I can wield cold cans (Baja Blast) as a weapon without eating the freeze myself.

## Context

Baja Blast is loot from US-017 and unlocks Bemidji Blizzard as a **spell**. Ideas.md separately: "can cozy — allows you to wield cold cans (baja blast)." That is a **melee/thrown wield**, not the spell cast.

Without the cozy, the can is too cold to hold as a weapon.

## Independent Test

Hold Baja Blast without a cozy: wield action is rejected (spell unlock from US-017 is unaffected). Pick up the can cozy, then wield Baja Blast: a cold melee or thrown attack applies a movement slow to the hit Paper Pusher. Dropping the cozy removes the ability to wield.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Given** a generated dungeon, **When** generation completes, **Then** a can cozy pickup exists in a reachable cell.
2. **Given** the DM has Baja Blast but no cozy, **When** they try to wield or throw the can as a weapon, **Then** the attack does not occur (feedback: too cold).
3. **Given** the DM has the cozy equipped or in inventory and has Baja Blast, **When** they wield, **Then** they perform a cold-can melee or short-range throw.
4. **Given** a cold-can hit lands on a Paper Pusher, **When** damage/slow resolves, **Then** the target takes damage and/or a brief movement slow (suggested 1.5s at 50% speed) distinct from a full Bemidji Blizzard.
5. **Given** the DM has the cozy, **When** they cast Bemidji Blizzard, **Then** the spell still follows US-017 (cozy is not a spell requirement).
6. **Given** the DM loses the cozy, **When** they try to wield again, **Then** wielding is blocked once more.
7. **Given** a Paper Pusher, **When** they touch the cozy, **Then** they do not equip it (DM-only).

## Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: Can cozy MUST be a DM-only dungeon pickup.
- **FR-002**: Wielding Baja Blast as a weapon MUST require the cozy on the server.
- **FR-003**: A successful wield hit MUST apply a short slow and modest damage (suggested 1–2 damage).
- **FR-004**: Wield MUST NOT consume the Baja Blast can (it remains a cube ingredient) unless a thrown mode is explicitly chosen; default is unconsumed melee wield.
- **FR-005**: Other Dew flavors are not wieldable as cold weapons unless later specified.
- **FR-006**: Hits MUST be host-authoritative.

## Multiplayer Requirements

- **MR-001**: Cozy ownership and wield hits MUST be server-authored.
- **MR-002**: Peers MUST see the wield swing/throw.

## Edge Cases

- Wield inside the dungeon against monsters: allowed; slow on monsters is optional (default: damage only to monsters, slow only to Paper Pushers).
- Blizzard active and wield slow stacked: use the stronger slow, do not multiply to zero speed.

## Key Entities

- **Can Cozy**: Insulating item required to wield cold cans.
- **Baja Blast Can**: Cold can weapon when cozy is held.

## Assumptions

- "Wield" is melee by default so the can survives for US-019.
- Green Dew and Code Red do not need the cozy.

## Out of Scope

- Bemidji Blizzard spell body (US-017).
- Cube recipe (US-019).
