# US-015: Asymmetric dungeon start and exit

**Status**: Draft  
**Source**: `ideas.md` — Dungeon, Pull start (timing), Paper Pushers  
**Priority**: P1  
**Roles**: Dungeon Master, Paper Pusher

## User Story

As the Dungeon Master, I start trapped in a dungeon I must explore, level up, and exit, so that Paper Pushers have time to harvest and build before I can fight them directly.

## Context

Procedural dungeon generation already produces a connected start→exit layout with rooms, hallways, and catalog monsters (`specs/001-procedural-dungeon-generator`). The playground currently drops both roles into a shared space. Ideas.md: the DM starts in the dungeon, finds power-ups, then after exiting can interact and fight more directly with Paper Pushers — "this gives time for the paper pushers to build up."

Dungeon pickups and bosses are US-016–US-018. Direct combat after exit is enabled here; the endgame abilities are US-021 and US-022. The dungeon is dungeon tiles; the Paper Pusher field is **outside** grass/dirt (US-023). Those catalogs must not mix.

## Independent Test

Start a match: DM spawns at the dungeon entrance and cannot yet attack the Reality Zone players as if they were in the overworld. Paper Pushers spawn in the Reality Zone and can harvest/build. DM reaches the exit, leaves, and can then move into the overworld/Reality Zone and fight. Duration of the dungeon crawl is long enough for at least one factory to be placed in a normal playtest.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Given** a match starts, **When** roles spawn, **Then** the Dungeon Master is placed at the dungeon entrance and Paper Pushers are placed in the Reality Zone, not in the dungeon.
2. **Given** the DM has not used the dungeon exit, **When** they try to leave the dungeon bounds by any path other than the exit, **Then** they remain inside the dungeon (walls / bounds hold).
3. **Given** Paper Pushers during the pre-exit phase, **When** they play, **Then** they can move in the Reality Zone, harvest, and build even while the DM is still inside.
4. **Given** the DM is still in the dungeon, **When** they encounter the Paper Pusher overworld, **Then** they cannot yet melee/spell that overworld as a shared battlefield (they are not physically there).
5. **Given** the DM reaches the dungeon exit and confirms leaving, **When** the server accepts the exit, **Then** the DM is placed onto **outside** tiles (US-023) in the overworld/Fantasy-adjacent space where they can path into the Reality Zone (US-001) and fight Paper Pushers directly.
6. **Given** the DM has exited, **When** they re-approach the dungeon, **Then** they may re-enter through the exit unless a later rule seals it (default: re-entry allowed).
7. **Given** a Paper Pusher tries to enter the dungeon, **When** movement is resolved, **Then** they are blocked (dungeon interior is Fantasy turf for this purpose, consistent with US-003).

## Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: Match start MUST spawn the DM at the generated dungeon entrance and Paper Pushers at Reality Zone spawn points.
- **FR-002**: The generated dungeon MUST include a usable exit distinct from the entrance (existing generator contract).
- **FR-003**: Until the DM uses the exit, they MUST be confined to the dungeon play space.
- **FR-004**: Paper Pushers MUST be able to run the office loop (US-005–US-010) in parallel during the dungeon crawl.
- **FR-005**: After a successful exit, the DM MUST be able to move on overworld **outside** tiles (US-023), enter the Reality Zone, and use unlocked spells/summons against Paper Pushers and buildings, subject to mana (US-014) and unlocks.
- **FR-006**: Exit MUST be host-authoritative and replicated (all peers see the DM in the overworld).
- **FR-007**: Dungeon monsters, pickups, and bosses that have not been consumed remain in the dungeon if the DM re-enters.
- **FR-008**: The dungeon interior MUST use dungeon catalog tiles only; the overworld play space MUST use outside grass/dirt tiles only. Exit MUST land the DM on an outside cell, not on a dungeon floor cell that was never replaced.

## Multiplayer Requirements

- **MR-001**: Spawn positions MUST be assigned by the host/server.
- **MR-002**: The DM's exited vs still-in-dungeon state MUST be the same for every peer.

## Edge Cases

- DM dies in the dungeon before exiting: respawn at the dungeon entrance, not in the Reality Zone.
- DM dies after exiting: respawn at a Fantasy-safe point (inside the Fantasy home rectangle or at the dungeon exit), not among Paper Pusher spawns.
- Generation failure: match must not start with the DM in the Reality Zone as a silent fallback; surface the failure.

## Key Entities

- **Dungeon Entrance**: DM spawn.
- **Dungeon Exit**: One-way (first-time) gate from dungeon tiles onto outside tiles.
- **Pre-exit Phase**: Paper Pusher build-up window on the overworld.

## Assumptions

- One dungeon per match, generated at start.
- "Levels up" during the crawl is covered by pickups, bosses, and Fantasy Level in US-016–US-018, not a separate XP bar, unless those stories add one.
- Suggested design target: a first-time dungeon crawl of a few minutes, not a full campaign.

## Out of Scope

- Specific power-ups (US-016), Baja Blast boss (US-017), Code Red dragon (US-018).
- Dad All Powerful (US-021) and chainsaw (US-022).
- Outside tile varieties and zone art drift (US-023, US-002, US-004).

## Required New Art Assets

Dungeon floors/walls already exist (`sprites/cubicle_stone_floor.png`, `cubicle_stone_wall.png`, **128×128** cells, offset `(0, -63)`). Entrance and exit are not distinct tiles today.
