# US-012: Knightlings randomly kill Paper Pushers

**Status**: Draft  
**Source**: `ideas.md` — monsters  
**Priority**: P2  
**Roles**: Dungeon Master, Paper Pusher

## User Story

As the Dungeon Master, I send knightlings that can randomly hit Paper Pushers and kill them so that office players never feel safe just because they are at full health.

## Context

A knight monster already exists with a blitz-charge state. Spawn is already on the DM HUD (`spawn_knight`). Ideas.md calls them **knightlings** and specifies they can **randomly hit players and kill them** — lethal, not a fair DPS race.

Unlock of knightling summon from green Mt Dew is US-016. Mana cost is US-014.

## Independent Test

Spawn a knightling near a full-health Paper Pusher. Over a short fight the knightling performs a strike that is either a miss/normal hit or a lethal hit. A lethal hit kills the player from any remaining HP. Death replicates and the player follows the existing respawn flow.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Given** a living knightling and a living Paper Pusher in its aggro/attack range, **When** the knightling executes a strike, **Then** the strike rolls a lethal check on the server.
2. **Given** the lethal check succeeds, **When** the hit is applied, **Then** the Paper Pusher dies regardless of remaining HP (subject to existing invulnerability frames).
3. **Given** the lethal check fails, **When** the hit is applied, **Then** the Paper Pusher takes normal knightling damage and may survive.
4. **Given** the Paper Pusher is invulnerable (`invulnerable == true`), **When** a lethal strike lands, **Then** it does not kill them.
5. **Given** a lethal kill occurs, **When** peers observe the player, **Then** they see the death and the existing respawn handling runs (Reality Zone spawn).
6. **Given** no Paper Pushers are in range, **When** the knightling wanders, **Then** it does not randomly kill the DM or goblins.

## Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: Knightlings MUST target Paper Pushers, not buildings (building raids are US-011).
- **FR-002**: Each knightling melee/blitz that actually hits a Paper Pusher MUST roll a configurable lethal chance (suggested default 15%).
- **FR-003**: A successful lethal roll MUST apply fatal damage (HP to 0) unless the target is invulnerable.
- **FR-004**: A failed lethal roll MUST apply normal attack damage only.
- **FR-005**: Lethal rolls MUST be host-authoritative and must not be rerolled independently on clients.
- **FR-006**: Knightlings MUST NOT apply the lethal roll to the Dungeon Master.
- **FR-007**: Summoning availability is gated by US-016 (green Dew unlock) and paid with US-014 (mana); this story owns combat behavior.

## Multiplayer Requirements

- **MR-001**: The lethal outcome MUST be identical for all peers (one server roll).
- **MR-002**: Death and respawn MUST use the existing DeathSystem / Reality Zone spawn path.

## Edge Cases

- Two knightlings hit the same player in one frame: at most one death processing; HP cannot go negative in a way that double-respawns.
- Lethal roll on a player with 1 HP: they die either way; still count as a lethal strike for telemetry if the roll succeeded.
- Player in a channel (filling a form): lethal death interrupts the channel (US-009).

## Key Entities

- **Knightling**: Player-hunting monster with a random execute.
- **Lethal Check**: Server RNG on a successful hit.

## Assumptions

- Existing knight scene is the knightling unless a smaller variant is added; behavior matters more than scale.
- "Randomly hit and kill" is an on-hit execute chance, not a random instant kill while the monster is off-screen.

## Out of Scope

- Unlock and mana (US-016, US-014).
- Goblin factory raids (US-011).

## Required New Art Assets

None required. Reuse `monsters/knight/lightning_knight.png` (**32×32** frames) and `monsters/knight/knight.png` for HUD. Optional lethal-hit flash: 32×32, 3–4 frames, same scale as `spells/fireball/fireball.png` — only if the execute is otherwise unreadable.
