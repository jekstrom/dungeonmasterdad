# US-028: Baja Blast fountain slick

**Status**: Draft  
**Source**: James — rework Freeze Wave into a fountain environmental  
**Priority**: P2  
**Roles**: Dungeon Master

## User Story

As the Dungeon Master exploring the dungeon, I find a water fountain that periodically gushes Baja Blast Mt Dew around its room so the floor goes slick and a splash can knock me back — the same Freeze Wave feel, but it reads as a room hazard, not a boss special.

## Context

This **replaces** the Baja Blast **Freeze Wave boss move**. The player-facing effect stays: a readable tell, a Baja splash that **knocks back** on overlap, then a **slippery dew sheet** on the floor (reduced friction / slide). The **source is a water fountain doodad** in the dungeon. It is **not** a Baja Blast boss state, **not** Carbonated Jet (US-027), **not** Sugar Rush (US-029), and **not** a Bemidji Blizzard Fantasy pocket (US-017 T005).

Host-authoritative. Period, charge, slick duration, and slick size are configurable. Paper Pusher occupancy T011 is unchanged: slide is friction, not a zone wall, and knockback is combat displacement, not a push-out of Fantasy.

Place **one fountain per generated dungeon** on a reachable walkable cell in the **same room as the Baja Blast boss** (the exit room). It MUST NOT be the entrance or exit cell, and SHOULD NOT occupy the boss tile when another cell in that room is free. Tests may skip the fountain.

## Independent Test

Walk into a dungeon room that has a water fountain. After a pause it **charges** (readable gurgle / swell), then **splashes Baja dew around that room**. Standing in the splash: hit + knockback. The room floor goes **slick**; walking on it slides. The slick expires and traction returns; the fountain splashes again later. A second window matches splash, knockback, and slick rect. The Baja Blast boss does **not** fire this as a move.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Given** a generated dungeon without skip-fountain, **When** generation completes, **Then** there is exactly one water fountain on a walkable cell that is not the entrance or exit.
2. **Given** the fountain is live, **When** its period elapses, **Then** a readable charge plays before any splash exists.
3. **Given** the splash fires, **When** it overlaps the DM, **When** the host resolves, **Then** the DM takes a hit and knockback.
4. **Given** the splash, **When** it lands, **Then** a Baja dew slick covers the fountain's room floor (configurable size/duration); players on it have reduced friction and slide.
5. **Given** Carbonated Jet or a boss melee, **When** the fountain splashes, **Then** it is a different system (doodad + room slick, not a piercing stream and not a boss clip).
6. **Given** two windows, **When** a splash happens, **Then** both see the same fountain, the same knockback outcome, and the same slick rect / remaining time.

## Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: The dungeon MUST spawn **exactly one** water fountain doodad per match (unless skip-fountain). Cell MUST be walkable and MUST NOT be `entrance_cell` or `exit_cell`.
- **FR-002**: The fountain MUST periodically **charge** then **splash Baja Blast Mt Dew around its room**. Charge MUST be readable before the splash. Suggested period 6–10s, charge ~1s.
- **FR-003**: On splash overlap, the host MUST apply a hit and **knockback**. Knockback MUST be host-authoritative. It MUST NOT shove anyone out of Fantasy as occupancy (T011).
- **FR-004**: After a splash, the host MUST spawn a **dew slick** on the fountain room's floor. Duration and size MUST be configurable (suggested duration 4–8s; size the room walkable cells or a large rect of 128px cells around the fountain). No wall hit is required — this is an environmental puddle, not a projectile that has to collapse on a wall.
- **FR-005**: The slick MUST reduce player friction so they **slide**. It is dungeon floor VFX + physics, not a Fantasy pocket and not Bemidji Blizzard slow (US-017 T005 50% move lock).
- **FR-006**: Charge, splash, knockback, slick spawn/expire MUST be host-authoritative and replicated. Late join during a live slick MUST receive the current rect and remaining time.
- **FR-007**: This story MUST NOT add a Freeze Wave / wave state to `baja_boss`. The boss keeps melee + Carbonated Jet (US-027) + Sugar Rush (US-029). Missing fountain art MUST NOT fail dungeon generation (placeholder doodad is allowed until art lands).

## Multiplayer Requirements

- **MR-001**: Fountain period, splash, knockback, and dew slick MUST be server-authored.
- **MR-002**: Peers MUST observe the same fountain cell, the same knockback outcome, and the same slick rect.

## Edge Cases

- Fountain room is open (no nearby wall): still splash and still spawn a slick (unlike the old wall-collapse Freeze Wave).
- DM standing on the fountain cell: collide with the doodad like other dungeon doodads; splash can still hit them if they are in range.
- Fountain / slick under the DM: they slide; they are not pushed out of the dungeon (T011 unchanged).
- Slick expires: traction baseline; next period may splash again.
- Boss dies: fountain keeps cycling; do not leave a **permanent** slick (expire on timer).
- Two splashes overlapping in time: refresh or replace the slick; do not stack infinite duration.
- Jet stream and fountain splash MUST NOT share the same projectile scene.
- Skip-fountain: generation succeeds with **zero** fountains and no slick.

## Key Entities

- **Water fountain**: Dungeon doodad. Source of the environmental splash. Not a boss.
- **Baja splash**: Periodic room burst of Mt Dew from the fountain. Hit + knockback on overlap.
- **Dew slick**: Temporary low-friction floor patch in the fountain's room after a splash. Same slide as the old icy sheet; Baja teal, not ice.

## Assumptions

- One fountain per dungeon is enough to read as a room hazard on the crawl.
- Place the fountain in the Baja Blast boss room (exit room) so the splash is an arena hazard. Do not occupy the boss tile when another cell in that room is free.
- Slick is friction, not a slow zone. Blizzard pocket remains US-017.

## Out of Scope

- Carbonated Jet (US-027), Sugar Rush (US-029) as **boss moves**.
- A Freeze Wave / wave clip on the Baja Blast boss.
- US-019 cube, US-020 cozy, US-018 fireball.
- Bemidji Blizzard Fantasy pocket (US-017 T005).
- PP occupancy (US-003 T011).
- Pickup cans (`pickups/bajablast/`) — this is floor dew from a fountain, not the droppable can.
