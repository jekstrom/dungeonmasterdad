# US-028: Baja Blast Freeze Wave

**Status**: Draft  
**Source**: James — 3 Baja Blast specials  
**Priority**: P2  
**Roles**: Dungeon Master

## User Story

As the Dungeon Master fighting the Baja Blast boss, I see a telegraphed Baja wave that knocks me back on hit, and if it slams a wall it becomes a large icy sheet I slide on.

## Context

Builds on US-017 boss combat. Distinct from US-027 Carbonated Jet (piercing linear stream). Sugar Rush is US-029. Host-authoritative. Duration and size of the ice sheet are configurable. PP occupancy T011 is unchanged.

## Independent Test

Aggro the boss. It telegraphs, then sends a Baja wave forward. On a DM hit: knockback. When the wave hits a dungeon wall: it collapses into a large icy sheet; walking on it slides (reduced friction). A second window matches wave, knockback, and ice.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Given** the boss chooses Freeze Wave, **When** the windup starts, **Then** a telegraph is readable before the wave fires.
2. **Given** the wave overlaps the DM, **When** the host resolves, **Then** the DM takes a hit and knockback.
3. **Given** the wave hits a wall, **When** it collapses, **Then** a large icy sheet appears; players on it have reduced friction and slide.
4. **Given** Carbonated Jet, **When** Freeze Wave plays, **Then** it is a different move (wave + ice sheet, not a piercing stream).

## Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: Freeze Wave MUST be telegraphed, then send a Baja wave **forward** from the boss.
- **FR-002**: On hit, the host MUST apply damage (or hit) and **knockback**. Knockback MUST be host-authoritative.
- **FR-003**: When the wave hits a **wall**, it MUST collapse into a **large icy sheet**. Duration and size MUST be configurable.
- **FR-004**: The icy sheet MUST reduce player friction so they **slide**. It is dungeon floor VFX + physics, not a Fantasy pocket and not Bemidji Blizzard.
- **FR-005**: Wave, knockback, sheet spawn/expire MUST be host-authoritative and replicated.

## Multiplayer Requirements

- **MR-001**: Wave, knockback, and ice sheet MUST be server-authored.
- **MR-002**: Peers MUST observe the same sheet rect and the same knockback outcome.

## Edge Cases

- Wave never hits a wall (open room): no sheet, or sheet at max range — default: no sheet unless a wall was hit.
- Boss dies mid-wave: wave may finish or despawn; do not leave a permanent ice sheet.
- Ice sheet under the DM: they slide; they are not pushed out of the dungeon (T011 unchanged).
- Jet and Freeze Wave must not share the same projectile scene.

## Key Entities

- **Freeze Wave**: Telegraphed forward Baja wave.
- **Icy sheet**: Temporary low-friction floor patch after a wall hit.

## Assumptions

- Suggested sheet duration 4–8s; size a large rect or several 128 cells in front of the wall.
- Carbonated Jet is implemented first (US-027).

## Out of Scope

- Carbonated Jet (US-027), Sugar Rush (US-029).
- US-019 cube, US-020 cozy, US-018 fireball.
- Bemidji Blizzard Fantasy pocket (US-017 T005) — this ice is dungeon floor slide, not a zone pocket.
- PP occupancy (US-003 T011).
