# US-025: Zone homes do not overlap

**Status**: Draft  
**Source**: James play report (Fantasy art outside Fantasy claim) + Game Director no-overlap rule  
**Priority**: P1  
**Roles**: Paper Pusher, Dungeon Master

## User Story

As a player in the match, I see Reality and Fantasy **homes** as two turfs that never share cells: the stronger zone pushes the weaker home back, and tile art always matches who currently claims the ground.

## Context

US-001 and US-003 currently **allow** overlapping homes (FR-010): Fantasy exclusion and the Reality skeleton ban both apply on the same cells. US-002 FR-007 and US-004 T004 currently **allow** overlapping homes for drift: higher covering level wins the claim; **ties keep current art**. That tie rule is why Fantasy presentation can linger on cells that are not Fantasy-claimed.

This story **supersedes** those home-overlap clauses. Homes become exclusive rectangles. Pockets still override homes for occupancy and drift while they last (US-001 FR-004, US-003 FR-004). Entire-map-one-zone as a game-over is **not** this story.

Reality home stays west-anchored (US-001). Fantasy home stays east-anchored (US-003). Inclusion is **center-point**, same as US-002 / US-004.

## Independent Test

Grow Reality and Fantasy homes until they would meet. They abut; they do not share cells. Raise one zone’s level: that home takes the contested cells and the weaker home rect shrinks off them. Equal levels: neither home advances into the other. Cast a pocket over the other home: occupancy and drift follow the pocket; when it expires, homes are still the no-overlap rects. A cell that loses Fantasy claim must not keep Fantasy grass/dirt art.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Given** Reality and Fantasy home rectangles, **When** claim is queried for any cell, **Then** at most one home covers that cell.
2. **Given** the two homes would meet or overlap, **When** zone values differ, **Then** the higher value keeps the contested cells and the weaker home rect shrinks so it no longer covers them.
3. **Given** the two homes would meet or overlap, **When** zone values are equal, **Then** neither home advances into the other (stable frontier). Contested cells are not dual-claimed.
4. **Given** a live pocket, **When** occupancy or drift is evaluated, **Then** the pocket still overrides homes for that duration. **When** it expires, **Then** remaining claim uses the no-overlap home rects.
5. **Given** an outside tile whose current presentation does not match its current claim, **When** claim is applied, **Then** that presentation is not allowed to linger: no Fantasy art outside Fantasy claim, no Reality art outside Reality claim.
6. **Given** one home would cover the whole map, **When** pushback runs, **Then** this story still only resolves rects and claim; it does not trigger game over.

## Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: Reality and Fantasy **homes** MUST NOT occupy the same cells. Adjacent (sharing an edge, not a cell) is allowed. Center-point inclusion: a cell belongs to at most one home.
- **FR-002**: After any home rebuild (level change, clip, load), the server MUST resolve overlap. Higher **zone value** (Reality Level vs Fantasy Level) wins every contested cell. The weaker home rectangle MUST shrink so those cells are outside it. Keep the weaker home’s spawn-side anchor: Reality shrinks from the east; Fantasy shrinks from the west. Vertical overflow shrinks the overlapping north/south edge, still keeping the west/east anchor.
- **FR-003**: Equal zone values: neither home MAY grow into cells the other home currently occupies. If they already overlap (old overlap rule or a glitch), both MUST retract from the intersection so no cell stays dual-covered. Tile-snap the split; if the contested band has an odd width, the leftover middle cell is unclaimed by either home.
- **FR-004**: Pockets still override homes while live (newer pocket wins). Pocket create/expire MUST NOT be the thing that shrinks a home. After expire, homes use the no-overlap rects from FR-001–FR-003.
- **FR-005**: Outside-tile **presentation** MUST follow current claim. Fantasy-element art is legal only on Fantasy-claimed cells. Reality-element art is legal only on Reality-claimed cells. Unclaimed cells MUST be Neutral. Stale linger until the other zone’s drift delay fires is forbidden. Convert-into-a-look may still use US-002 / US-004 stagger **after** the cell is already claimed; losing a claim MUST drop that zone’s art immediately (snap to Neutral, or to the new claim’s art if that claim is already applied).
- **FR-006**: Resolved home rects MUST be host-authoritative and replicated, including to late joiners.
- **FR-007**: This story MUST NOT implement a game-over or match-end when one zone covers the map.

## Multiplayer Requirements

- **MR-001**: Overlap resolve, shrink, and presentation correction MUST originate on the host/server.
- **MR-002**: All peers MUST observe the same home rectangles and the same outside-tile presentation after resolve.

## Edge Cases

- Homes grow in the same frame: resolve once per rebuild, not a fight per physics frame.
- Weaker home shrinks onto a building that was fully inside it: existing buildings are not auto-destroyed (US-001 / US-003); new placement still uses the live claim.
- Weaker home shrinks over a Paper Pusher into Fantasy: US-003 displacement applies.
- Contested cells that are dungeon: occupancy may still apply; this story does not restyle dungeon catalog tiles (US-023).
- Pocket over the other home: cells are pocket-claimed; home rects stay no-overlap underneath.
- Odd-width equal overlap: one unclaimed frontier cell is OK; dual-claim is not.

## Key Entities

- **Zone value**: Reality Level vs Fantasy Level, used only to decide who wins contested **home** cells.
- **Home frontier**: Exclusive abutting edge between the two home rects. Not a pocket.
- **Claim**: Unchanged pocket-overrides-home model, now with exclusive homes underneath.

## Assumptions

- One Reality home and one Fantasy home per match.
- Overlap is expected to be a band between west Reality and east Fantasy, not a home punched through the other.
- US-002 / US-004 delay ranges still apply to converting **into** a claimed look.

## Out of Scope

- Game over / win when one zone covers the map.
- Occupancy rules themselves (US-001, US-003), except that homes no longer overlap.
- Catalog membership (US-023).
- Pocket geometry and blizzard slow (US-017).
- How zone levels are earned (US-008, US-009).

## Required New Art Assets

None. Overlays stay US-001 / US-003. Ground variants stay US-023. Drift puffs stay US-002 / US-004.
