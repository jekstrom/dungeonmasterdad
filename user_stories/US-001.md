# US-001: Reality Zone play space

**Status**: Draft  
**Source**: `ideas.md` — reality zone  
**Priority**: P1  
**Roles**: Paper Pusher, Dungeon Master

## User Story

As a Paper Pusher, I hold a rectangular Reality home turf I can move and build in (the Dungeon Master can enter it too), and I can create temporary rectangular pockets of Reality, so the office is our world — including brief beachheads punched into Fantasy.

## Context

This story defines **occupancy and content rules** for Reality. Shape is a design choice: **axis-aligned rectangles by default**, not the current circular `Area2D` radius.

The map has:

- **Home region**: one persistent Reality rectangle that grows with Reality Level.
- **Pockets**: additional rectangles created by effects, with a duration. While a pocket is live it is Reality for every rule in this story.

The ground under overworld Reality is **outside tiles** (grass/dirt, US-023). Tile art drift is US-002. Fantasy occupancy is US-003. Pockets override home coverage while they last (see claim rules below). The Reality home (and all pockets) MUST sit inside the **map interior** (US-024); Paper Pushers spawn on the left edge of that interior.

## Independent Test

Stand in a rectangular Reality home: Paper Pushers walk it; the DM can walk in; a legal building places; a skeleton that enters is removed. Grow the rectangle with Reality Level and confirm newly covered skeletons die. Spawn a temporary Reality pocket over Fantasy-covered ground: Paper Pushers may occupy that pocket, buildings may place if fully inside it, skeletons in it die. When the pocket expires, Fantasy rules return on that ground (US-003) and Paper Pushers still inside Fantasy home are pushed out.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Given** a Paper Pusher is inside Reality (home or pocket), **When** they move, **Then** movement is unrestricted by zone type (normal collision with walls, buildings, and doodads still applies).
2. **Given** a Dungeon Master is outside Reality, **When** they walk into it, **Then** they enter freely and remain controllable inside it.
3. **Given** a Paper Pusher has the required resources and a valid clear footprint fully inside Reality, on outside tiles, **When** they confirm placement, **Then** the building is created and enabled.
4. **Given** a building footprint that is not fully inside Reality, **When** placement is requested, **Then** the server rejects it and no building is created.
5. **Given** a skeleton is alive, **When** its position is inside Reality (home or pocket), **Then** the skeleton cannot remain: it is destroyed or despawned on the server and all peers see it gone.
6. **Given** a skeleton would spawn inside Reality, **When** the spawn is evaluated, **Then** the spawn is rejected.
7. **Given** the Reality home rectangle grows, **When** an existing skeleton is newly covered, **Then** that skeleton is removed the same as if it had walked in.
8. **Given** a Paper Pusher effect that creates a Reality pocket, **When** the effect resolves, **Then** a rectangular Reality region appears for a finite duration and all occupancy rules in this story apply inside it, including over ground that was Fantasy home.
9. **Given** a live Reality pocket expires, **When** the next occupancy pass runs, **Then** that rectangle is no longer Reality by itself; remaining home/pocket coverage is re-evaluated and actors/buildings follow the resulting claim (existing buildings are not auto-destroyed).

## Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: Reality coverage MUST be the union of the Reality **home region** and all live Reality **pockets**. Default region shape is an **axis-aligned rectangle** (optionally snapped to the tile grid). Circles MUST NOT be the default.
- **FR-002**: The home region MUST persist for the match and MUST grow as Reality Level increases (suggested: expand width and/or height, or each edge, by a configurable amount per level). Occupancy MUST use the live rectangle, not the size at session start. Growth MUST clip to the map interior (US-024); the home MUST NOT cross the cliff ring. Suggested start: anchored on the **west** interior edge (Paper Pusher spawn).
- **FR-003**: Effects MAY create Reality pockets: extra rectangles with an origin, size, and duration. Suggested default: tile-aligned rect, duration on the effect (e.g. 6–12s). Specific Paper Pusher abilities that spawn pockets are not named here; any effect tagged as a Reality pocket MUST use this contract.
- **FR-004**: While a pocket is live, every point inside it MUST be treated as Reality for movement, building, skeletons, and (via US-002) tile claim, **even if a Fantasy home region also covers that point**. Pockets override homes. If two pockets overlap, the **newer** pocket wins.
- **FR-005**: Paper Pushers MUST be allowed to path and occupy any walkable cell that is Reality-claimed.
- **FR-006**: The Dungeon Master MUST be allowed to path and occupy walkable cells that are Reality-claimed.
- **FR-007**: Building placement MUST be allowed only when the entire footprint lies inside Reality-claimed area, on **outside** tiles (US-023), and the placement area is otherwise clear. Dungeon cells MUST NOT accept buildings via this path.
- **FR-008**: Skeletons MUST be forbidden from existing in Reality-claimed area, including by walking in, being pushed in, spawning in, or a home/pocket appearing over them.
- **FR-009**: Skeleton removal and pocket create/expire MUST be host-authoritative and replicated to every peer.
- **FR-010**: Where Reality and Fantasy **homes** overlap and **no pocket** covers the point, Fantasy exclusion still wins for Paper Pushers (US-003) and the Reality skeleton ban still applies. Buildings still require a footprint fully in Reality and not intersecting Fantasy (overlap rejects placement).

## Multiplayer Requirements

- **MR-001**: Home rectangle, pocket set, building placement success/failure, and skeleton removal MUST be decided by the host/server.
- **MR-002**: All peers MUST observe the same Reality home rectangle, the same live pockets, and the same set of buildings and living skeletons.

## Edge Cases

- Reality home shrinks (if Reality Level can drop) and a building ends up partially outside: existing buildings remain; new placements still require a fully-inside footprint.
- Skeleton dies in the same frame it crosses a Reality edge or a pocket appears over it.
- Reality home overlaps dungeon bounds: occupancy still applies to actors; dungeon cells keep dungeon tiles and MUST NOT gain outside grass/dirt (US-023). Zone art drift does not restyle dungeon floors (US-002).
- Reality pocket under a Paper Pusher in Fantasy: they may enter/remain for the pocket duration; on expire, US-003 displacement applies if they are still in Fantasy-claimed ground.
- Reality pocket over an existing factory: the factory is not destroyed; it is simply in Reality (or returns to Fantasy-claimed ground when the pocket ends).
- Pocket whose rectangle is degenerate or zero-size: treat as no pocket.

## Key Entities

- **Reality Home Region**: Persistent axis-aligned rectangle that grows with Reality Level.
- **Reality Pocket**: Temporary rectangle of Reality created by an effect.
- **Reality Claim**: A point is Reality-claimed if a live Reality pocket covers it (and wins overlap) or else the Reality home covers it under FR-010.
- **Reality Level**: Shared Paper Pusher progression value that expands the home region.
- **Skeleton**: Fantasy-only monster that cannot exist in Reality-claimed space.

## Assumptions

- One Reality home region exists per match, inside the map interior (US-024).
- Buildings already placed are not auto-destroyed if coverage later fails.
- Goblins, knightlings, and gremlins are not banned by this story (only skeletons).
- Paper Pusher pocket-creating abilities may ship in a later story; this story still requires the pocket contract so those abilities have a place to plug in.

## Out of Scope

- Tile art swapping on outside grass/dirt (US-002).
- Outside vs dungeon catalog membership (US-023).
- Fantasy home, Fantasy pockets, and Paper Pusher exclusion (US-003).
- How Reality Level is earned (US-008, US-009).
- Named Paper Pusher pocket abilities (only the region contract).
- Map cliff geometry and overworld fill (US-024).

## Required New Art Assets

Pixel art, same 3/4 camera and south-foot y-sort as `level/floor.tscn`. Ground-sized assets are **128×128** cells like `sprites/cubicle_stone_floor.png`.

| Asset | Dimensions | Match | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reality home overlay | 128×128, tileable | Floor cell size; grey cubicle/office palette | Semi-transparent wash so grass/dirt still reads. Drawn over claimed cells, not a circle. |
| Reality pocket overlay | 128×128, tileable | Same cell size; paper-tape or dashed office border | Must be distinct from the home wash so temporary pockets are obvious. |

Do **not** reuse dungeon floor/wall frames as the overlay. Occupancy debug can stay code-drawn; these overlays are the player-facing look.
