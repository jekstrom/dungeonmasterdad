# US-007: Harvest iron from mines to build

**Status**: Draft  
**Source**: `ideas.md` — Paper Pushers  
**Priority**: P2  
**Roles**: Paper Pusher

## User Story

As a Paper Pusher, I harvest iron from mines so that I can pay the metal cost of buildings.

## Context

`BuildingData` already has `cost_item` and `cost_qty`. Metal exists as a pickup (`pickups/metal.tres`). There is no mine node and no guaranteed iron source on the map. Smoke and paper factories are the current buildables.

This story is the iron supply. Placement still requires the Reality Zone (US-001) and is banned in Fantasy (US-003).

## Independent Test

Locate a mine, harvest it, receive iron. Spend that iron on a legal building placement and confirm the cost is deducted and the building appears.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Given** a Paper Pusher is in range of an active mine, **When** they use the harvest action, **Then** mine harvest progress increases.
2. **Given** a mine harvest completes, **When** the server resolves it, **Then** iron is granted to inventory or dropped as a world pickup, and the mine remains a node (mines are not destroyed by one harvest).
3. **Given** a mine is depleted, **When** a Paper Pusher harvests, **Then** no iron is granted until the mine regenerates.
4. **Given** a player has enough iron for a building's `cost_qty`, **When** they place that building legally, **Then** iron is consumed and the building is created.
5. **Given** a player does not have enough iron, **When** they request placement of an iron-costing building, **Then** placement is rejected and no iron is consumed.
6. **Given** inventory is full, **When** a mine harvest completes, **Then** iron drops as a world pickup instead of vanishing.

## Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: The match MUST include one or more mine nodes in harvestable space outside the Fantasy Zone.
- **FR-002**: Mines MUST yield iron over repeated harvest actions, with a configurable hits-per-yield and yields-before-deplete.
- **FR-003**: Iron MUST be the `cost_item` (or equivalent) for buildings that require metal.
- **FR-004**: Depleted mines MUST optionally regenerate after a configurable cooldown; if cooldown is 0 they stay depleted.
- **FR-005**: Harvest and cost deduction MUST be host-authoritative.
- **FR-006**: Gremlins (US-013) MUST be able to interact with dropped iron the same as other world resources.

## Multiplayer Requirements

- **MR-001**: Concurrent harvesting of one mine MUST share server-side progress; yields are not duplicated.
- **MR-002**: All peers MUST see mine depleted/active state.

## Edge Cases

- Last iron spent on a placement that is then rejected for footprint reasons: cost MUST NOT be deducted (existing BuildingManager already checks resources and area together; keep that atomic).
- Mine overlapping Reality Zone growth: still harvestable if Paper Pushers can stand on it.

## Key Entities

- **Mine**: Repeatable harvest node.
- **Iron**: Building construction resource.

## Assumptions

- Suggested default: 4 hits → 1 iron; 5 iron per mine then cooldown.
- Smoke/paper factories keep using this iron cost unless a specific building is marked free.

## Out of Scope

- Wood/paper (US-006).
- Building combat damage (US-011).
- IRS / Office Max as unique buildings: they also cost iron unless marked otherwise in those stories.
