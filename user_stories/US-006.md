# US-006: Harvest wood from trees for paper

**Status**: Draft  
**Source**: `ideas.md` — Paper Pushers  
**Priority**: P2  
**Roles**: Paper Pusher

## User Story

As a Paper Pusher, I harvest wood from trees and feed it into a paper factory so that the office can produce paper.

## Context

Trees exist as `TreeDoodad` visuals. They are not harvestable and are not in the inventory loop. `PaperFactory` currently consumes smoke and raises Reality Level; it does not take wood or emit a paper item.

Paper factories raising Reality Level more than smoke factories is US-008. Turning paper into forms is US-009. Match start scatters trees on interior overworld cells (US-024); this story is harvest, not placement.

## Independent Test

Find a tree, harvest it, receive wood in inventory. Deliver wood to a paper factory that also has smoke (US-008). Factory consumes wood and produces paper in inventory or as a world pickup the harvester can take.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Given** a Paper Pusher is in range of a living tree, **When** they use the harvest action, **Then** the tree takes harvest damage and, after enough hits, is consumed.
2. **Given** a tree is fully harvested, **When** the server resolves it, **Then** wood is granted (inventory or world pickup) and the tree is removed or replaced with a stump that is not re-harvestable until respawn.
3. **Given** a Paper Pusher inventory is full, **When** a tree is harvested, **Then** wood drops as a world pickup instead of being lost.
4. **Given** a paper factory is enabled and has both required wood and required smoke, **When** a production interval completes, **Then** it consumes those inputs and produces paper.
5. **Given** a paper factory is missing wood, **When** a production interval elapses, **Then** it produces no paper and consumes no smoke for that tick.
6. **Given** another peer is present, **When** a tree is harvested, **Then** all peers see the tree gone and the same wood grant/drop.

## Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: Trees MUST be harvestable by Paper Pushers only, using a server-validated melee/harvest action.
- **FR-002**: Each tree MUST require a configurable number of harvest hits (suggested default 3) before yielding wood.
- **FR-003**: A fully harvested tree MUST yield a configurable amount of wood (suggested default 1–2) and then become unavailable.
- **FR-004**: Wood MUST be an inventory item Paper Pushers can carry and that paper factories accept as input.
- **FR-005**: Paper factories MUST require wood in addition to smoke (US-008) to produce paper.
- **FR-006**: Paper MUST be an inventory item (or world pickup that can be picked into inventory) distinct from wood.
- **FR-007**: Harvesting MUST NOT work from inside the Fantasy Zone (Paper Pushers cannot be there — US-003). Trees inside Fantasy, if any, MUST NOT be harvestable.
- **FR-008**: Tree removal, wood grants, and factory consumption MUST be host-authoritative.

## Multiplayer Requirements

- **MR-001**: Two players hitting the same tree MUST share one harvest progress bar on the server; wood is granted once.
- **MR-002**: Inventory wood counts MUST replicate to the owning player's HUD.

## Edge Cases

- Tree harvested on the same frame by two players' last hits: one yield, no duplicate wood.
- Paper factory production while the depositing player disconnects: consumption and output stay on the server; paper is not deleted.
- Tree under a placed building: cannot be harvested; building placement should prefer clear ground (existing clear-area check).

## Key Entities

- **Tree**: Harvest node in the Reality/overworld space.
- **Wood**: Input resource for paper factories.
- **Paper**: Output resource used by US-009.

## Assumptions

- Trees may respawn on a long timer; if they do not, the match has a finite wood supply.
- Feeding wood can be automatic when the player is in a factory deposit radius, or an explicit interact; interact-to-deposit is the default so gremlins (US-013) can contest world piles.

## Out of Scope

- Iron/mines (US-007).
- Forms and IRS (US-009).
- Reality Level amounts (US-008).
- Where trees are scattered on the map (US-024).

## Required New Art Assets

Living trees already exist (`sprites/tree.png`, **32×32** frames). Do not redraw the tree atlas unless adding a harvest-damaged frame.

| Asset | Dimensions | Match | Notes |

| Wood pickup | 32×32 | `pickups/coal/coal.png`, `pickups/metal/metal_01.png` | Log / lumber pile. Not dungeon stone. |
