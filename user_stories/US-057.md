# US-057: Generalized maze-shaped dungeon layouts

**Status**: Implemented  
**Source**: James — dungeon generator produces too many long “sausage” layouts; wants squares and other shapes, freer entrance/exit, more maze-like feel  
**Priority**: P1  
**Roles**: Dungeon Master, Paper Pusher

## User Story

As the Dungeon Master, when a match generates a dungeon, I crawl a **compact, maze-like** floor of rooms and winding hallways whose **overall shape can be square (or other compact footprints)**, not a long corridor between two poles — and the **entrance and exit can sit more freely** inside that footprint so each run feels like a maze, not a hallway.

## Context

Procedural generation already exists (`specs/001-procedural-dungeon-generator`, `DungeonGenerator` knobs, pipeline under `scripts/procedural_dungeon/generation/`). It **does** guarantee start→exit connectivity (`PathValidator` BFS) and rooms + hallways. In practice layouts feel **long**:

- `RoomGraphGenerator` places mid rooms on an **L-carve** (`DungeonGrid.carve_l`) from `start_cell` to `exit_cell`, then sorts them along the start→exit axis.
- `HallwayCarver` connects room centers with more **L-carves** (axis-aligned elbows). That is a shortest-axis path, not a maze.
- `MazeInfillGenerator` only grows a **few dead-end pockets** off existing hallways. It does not fill unused bounds with maze corridors.
- Inspector `start_cell` / `exit_cell` are treated as **room centers**. Typical playground values sit far apart on one axis, so the walkable AABB is a long rectangle even when `bounds_size` is square (e.g. 24×24 or 32×32).

US-015 still needs **one entrance and one distinct exit** with a usable overworld door. US-024 still commits the dungeon **AABB flush to the east interior edge**. This story changes **how cells are carved inside generation bounds**, not where the dungeon sits on the overworld.

Inspiration (procedural maze practice, not a mandate to copy one paper):

- **Rooms first, then corridors** — place rooms in the bounds, then connect them with winding 4-connected passages rather than L-shortest paths.
- **Growing Tree / recursive backtracker (DFS)** — long, twisting corridors with a strong “lost in a maze” read.
- **Wilson / Kruskal spanning tree** — unbiased or organic trees when we want less DFS “river” bias.
- **Braiding** — add a controlled number of extra loops so the maze is imperfect (not only one solution, not only dead ends).
- **Compact bounding box** — walkable cells should fill a **squarish or otherwise intended footprint**, not inherit the start–exit vector.
- **Solution length** — the shortest start→exit path should be **longer than Manhattan/L-distance** (winding), while still remaining one connected component.

## Open defaults (confirm with James)

| Topic | Default for v1 | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Footprint | Prefer **compact** walkable AABBs: square or near-square when bounds are square; elongated only when bounds themselves are elongated or a knob asks for it. | Stops “long dungeon” as the default. |
| Room placement | Scatter start, exit, and mid rooms **in the generation bounds** with existing size/separation rules. Do **not** require mid rooms to lie on the start–exit L. | Decouples shape from the two poles. |
| Hallways | Replace L-carve-as-default with **maze-inspired 4-connected carving** between rooms (growing tree or recursive backtracker on a corridor grid, or winding paths with jogs). Keep 4-connected (no diagonal corner-cut). | Matches occupancy / PathValidator / US-056. |
| Loops | Imperfect maze: spanning tree **plus braid**. Suggested braid: extra loops on ~15–30% of eligible dead-ends / adjacent corridor pairs (knob). | Pure perfect mazes feel frustrating in a combat crawl. |
| Entrance / exit | Any two distinct in-bounds cells that can host start/exit **rooms** (min Chebyshev separation as today). Not required to sit on opposite bounds edges or on the long axis. Exit **door** still needs a legal overworld landing (US-015 / US-024). | Freer placement; overworld contract stays. |
| Unused bounds | Optional maze **infill** of leftover cells (thin corridors / extra dead-ends) so the footprint reads as a maze, not rooms floating in a rectangle of void-then-walls. | Current infill is too sparse to feel mazelike. |
| Metrics (reject/retry) | After compose, if walkable AABB aspect (max/min side) **> 1.8** on square bounds, or shortest path length **< 1.4×** Chebyshev(start, exit), retry (existing `MAX_GENERATION_ATTEMPTS`). | Makes “not long” and “maze-like” testable. |
| Knobs | Keep `room_size`, `room_count`, bounds, start/exit. Add suggested: `layout_compactness` or `braid_rate`; start/exit may be **auto-picked** when a sentinel is used. | Inspector remains the layout surface. |
| Unchanged | Host-only generate; existing floor/wall tiles; monster/pickup/boss planners; PathValidator connectivity; clients do not generate. | Do not reopen US-016–018 content. |

## Independent Test

Generate with **square** bounds (e.g. 24×24) and several seeds. Walkable dungeon AABB is **not** a long sausage (aspect ≲ 1.8). Entrance and exit are **not** always opposite ends of one axis; they remain distinct rooms with a walkable route. Hallways **wind** (shortest path clearly longer than an L). There are **branches and/or loops**, not a single elbow corridor. Generate again with **non-square** bounds and confirm the generator can still fill that rectangle without forcing an L-backbone. PathValidator still finds start→exit. Existing start-room Dew / Code Red / bosses still place when those stories’ knobs are on.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Given** square (or near-square) generation bounds and a valid request, **When** generation succeeds, **Then** the walkable-cell AABB is compact (default: aspect ratio ≤ 1.8) and is **not** required to align with the start→exit vector.
2. **Given** the same bounds and different seeds, **When** several layouts are produced, **Then** overall footprints vary (square-ish blob, squat, irregular) and are not all long corridors.
3. **Given** start and exit cells inside bounds with legal room separation, **When** they are **not** on opposite edges, **Then** generation still succeeds (freer placement); entrance ≠ exit; both remain walkable.
4. **Given** a generated layout, **When** the shortest walkable start→exit path is measured, **Then** it is a **winding** 4-connected route (default: length ≥ 1.4× Chebyshev distance between room centers), not only `carve_l`.
5. **Given** that layout, **When** hallway topology is inspected, **Then** it reads as a maze: multiple branches and/or loops (braid), plus dead-ends — not a single L plus 1–3 pockets as the only extra structure.
6. **Given** PathValidator, **When** the layout is accepted, **Then** entrance and exit remain in one connected walkable component (existing connectivity contract).
7. **Given** US-015 / US-024, **When** the dungeon commits, **Then** the dungeon AABB is still east-flush on the interior; the exit still has a legal overworld landing; the DM still spawns at the entrance.
8. **Given** existing content planners (Dew, Code Red, dice, bosses, fountain), **When** this generator ships, **Then** they still receive start/exit rooms and walkable cells; they MUST NOT require the old L-backbone.
9. **Given** a joining client, **When** the host has generated, **Then** they see the host layout only (no second generate).

## Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: The layout builder MUST NOT use “place rooms on start→exit L, connect with L-carves” as the **only** or **default** topology.
- **FR-002**: Room placement MUST be able to scatter start, exit, and mid rooms inside `generation_bounds` subject to room size, min cells, and center separation (existing constants unless retuned).
- **FR-003**: Hallway generation MUST produce **4-connected** corridors inspired by maze algorithms (growing tree, recursive backtracker, or equivalent winding spanning tree). L-carve MAY remain a fallback if a maze carve fails, not the primary look.
- **FR-004**: The generator MUST support **imperfect** mazes: optional extra loops (braid) so more than one start→exit route can exist.
- **FR-005**: Entrance and exit MUST remain exactly one each, distinct, in-bounds, in rooms (or room-equivalent clusters), and PathValidator-connected. They MUST NOT be required to occupy opposite bounds edges.
- **FR-006**: When generation bounds are square, the walkable AABB SHOULD be compact (AC1 metric). Elongated bounds MAY yield elongated dungeons; the generator MUST still fill them with maze corridors rather than a single L.
- **FR-007**: Maze infill MUST be able to use leftover in-bounds cells (not only 1–3 dead-end pockets) so unused space can become corridors or extra rooms/alcoves.
- **FR-008**: Start/exit MAY be auto-placed when the request uses a documented sentinel (suggested: `DungeonGrid.SENTINEL` or a bool `auto_place_portals`) so designers are not forced to pick two far poles. Explicit cells still win when valid.
- **FR-009**: Retry/reject MUST use the same attempt budget as today if compactness or winding metrics fail.
- **FR-010**: Host authority, tile catalog, monster catalog, pickup/boss planners, and client non-generation stay as in 001 / US-015 / US-016. This story MUST NOT move the dungeon off the east interior edge (US-024).
- **FR-011**: Occupancy / US-056 MUST still see a coherent walkable set plus walls; no diagonal corner-cuts through walls.

## Multiplayer Requirements

- **MR-001**: Layout MUST be generated only on the host (`Lobby.is_network_server()` / existing manager).
- **MR-002**: All peers MUST observe the same rooms, hallways, entrance, and exit.

## Edge Cases

- Start and exit too close for two rooms: keep `LAYOUT_INFEASIBLE` / existing errors.
- Bounds 16×16 minimum: maze + rooms must still fit or fail closed.
- Auto-place portals vs explicit inspector cells: explicit valid pair is honored.
- Extra graph edge that currently L-carves a shortcut: replace with maze braid, not a second L.
- Exit door on a face that is not west-of-dungeon: still must land the DM on outside tiles (US-015); prefer a dungeon-perimeter cell adjacent to overworld.
- Very high braid rate: still 4-connected; do not open wall diagonals.
- Existing contract tests that assume L-backbone mid rooms: update or replace them in this story’s harness; do not silently keep the old backbone to satisfy stale asserts.

## Key Entities

- **Generation bounds**: Axis-aligned cell rect (existing). Shape of **walkable** cells is this story.
- **Room graph**: Start, mid, exit rooms; edges mean “connected by hallway,” not “L-carve these centers.”
- **Maze corridors**: 4-connected carved cells between and around rooms.
- **Braid**: Extra loops added after a spanning tree.
- **Entrance / exit**: Distinct portal cells, freely placed within the rules above.
- **Walkable AABB**: Bounding box of walkable dungeon cells; compactness metric.

## Assumptions

- Default room size/count knobs stay meaningful.
- “Mazelike” means winding corridors + branches/loops, not a perfect grid of 1-wide passages with no rooms.
- Overworld (cliffs, outside tiles, Reality west spawn) is unchanged.

## Out of Scope

- Moving the dungeon AABB off the east interior edge (US-024).
- Diagonal (8-connected) walking or corner-cutting.
- New floor/wall art.
- Changing mana, skills, or monster AI (US-056 still follows the new walkable set).
- Client-side generation.
- Fully organic blob caves (no rooms); rooms remain part of the crawl.

## Required New Art Assets

None. Existing `level/floor.tscn` and `level/wall.tscn` (and autotile) MUST suffice.
