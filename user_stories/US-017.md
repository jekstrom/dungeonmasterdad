# US-017: Baja Blast boss unlocks Bemidji Blizzard

**Status**: Draft  
**Source**: `ideas.md` — Dungeon  
**Priority**: P2  
**Roles**: Dungeon Master, Paper Pusher

## User Story

As the Dungeon Master, I defeat the Baja Blast boss in the dungeon to unlock Bemidji Blizzard, a spell that plants a temporary Fantasy pocket, slows factories, and slows Paper Pusher movement so that I can freeze a patch of the office into dungeon weather.

## Context

No Baja Blast boss exists. Code Red is the pattern for a flavor-can pickup that unlocks a spell. Ideas.md: Baja Blast boss → unlocks **Bemidji Blizzard**, which **slows down factories and player movement**. This story also uses the zone pocket contract (US-003): the blizzard's area **temporarily becomes Fantasy** — Paper Pushers cannot occupy it, new buildings cannot place there, skeletons may exist there, and outside tiles drift toward Fantasy art (US-004). Wielding the cold can as a weapon requires the can cozy (US-020). The can is also one of the three flavors for the cube (US-019).

## Independent Test

Reach the Baja Blast boss room, defeat the boss, receive the Baja Blast can / unlock. Cast Bemidji Blizzard with enough mana onto a rectangular patch of Reality home: that rectangle becomes a Fantasy pocket for the duration; Paper Pushers inside are pushed out and slowed if they remain adjacent/affected; factories in the pocket run slower; when the pocket expires, Reality occupancy returns and speeds return to baseline. The spell cannot be cast before the unlock or without mana.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Given** a generated dungeon, **When** generation completes, **Then** it contains a Baja Blast boss encounter in a reachable room that is not the entrance cell.
2. **Given** the DM deals the killing blow (or the boss reaches 0 HP), **When** the boss dies, **Then** a Baja Blast can is granted or dropped and Bemidji Blizzard is unlocked.
3. **Given** Blizzard is not unlocked, **When** the DM tries to cast it, **Then** nothing happens.
4. **Given** Blizzard is unlocked and the DM has enough mana (US-014), **When** they cast Bemidji Blizzard, **Then** mana is spent, a rectangular **Fantasy pocket** is created for the duration (US-003), and a slow effect starts in that area.
5. **Given** the Fantasy pocket is live, **When** occupancy is evaluated, **Then** all Fantasy occupancy rules apply inside it (Paper Pushers cannot remain, new buildings cannot place, skeletons may exist unless a newer Reality pocket covers the point).
6. **Given** the slow effect is active, **When** a Paper Pusher in or leaving the affected area moves, **Then** their move speed is reduced by a configurable factor (suggested 50% for the duration).
7. **Given** the slow effect is active, **When** an enabled factory in the pocket would complete a production interval, **Then** that interval takes longer (suggested 2× interval) for the duration.
8. **Given** the duration ends, **When** the next occupancy and movement/production tick runs, **Then** the Fantasy pocket is gone, speeds and intervals are back to baseline, and Reality home rules apply again if that rectangle is still Reality-claimed.
9. **Given** the DM has not exited the dungeon, **When** they unlock Blizzard, **Then** they may still cast it inside the dungeon (the pocket still applies; factories are usually in Reality — the spell is meant for after exit, but is not hard-gated on exit).

## Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: The dungeon MUST place exactly one Baja Blast boss per match unless generation explicitly skips bosses for tests.
- **FR-002**: Boss death MUST unlock `bemidji_blizzard` (or equivalent) via the DM unlock system and grant the Baja Blast can as a DM item/pickup.
- **FR-003**: Bemidji Blizzard MUST cost mana (suggested 30) and MUST NOT cast without unlock + mana.
- **FR-004**: A successful cast MUST create one **Fantasy pocket** (US-003): an axis-aligned rectangle (optionally tile-snapped) at the targeted area, lasting for the spell duration. Default shape is rectangular, not a circle.
- **FR-005**: The slow MUST apply to Paper Pusher movement and to factory production timers **inside that pocket**, not to goblin/knightling/gremlin movement unless later tuned.
- **FR-006**: Duration MUST be configurable (suggested 8s). Rectangle size MUST be configurable (suggested: a targeted ground rect using the same targeting pattern as fireball, not a radius).
- **FR-007**: Factory slow MUST affect smoke and paper factories whose origin lies inside the pocket. It MUST NOT silently slow every factory on the map.
- **FR-008**: When the duration ends, the server MUST remove the pocket in the same tick it restores speeds/intervals.
- **FR-009**: Effects MUST be host-authoritative and replicated (pocket geometry, occupancy, speed, and factory timers).

## Multiplayer Requirements

- **MR-001**: Unlock, cast, and slow MUST be server-authored.
- **MR-002**: All Paper Pusher clients in the slow MUST feel the same speed factor.

## Edge Cases

- Factory interval was 90% complete when slow starts: remaining time scales; do not reset progress to 0.
- Player respawns during blizzard: spawn at Reality spawn points (outside the pocket if it is Fantasy-claimed); do not spawn into the pocket.
- Blizzard over a factory: occupancy does not destroy it; it keeps producing at the slowed rate until the pocket ends.
- A newer Reality pocket overlapping the blizzard: newer pocket wins (US-001 / US-003); blizzard slow may still apply if the implementation ties slow to the spell instance rather than to remaining Fantasy claim — default: slow lasts for the spell duration on targets that were in the rect at cast, even if a Reality pocket later covers it.
- Boss pulled into Reality: still a dungeon boss; do not require Reality combat.

## Key Entities

- **Baja Blast Boss**: Dungeon encounter.
- **Baja Blast Can**: Unlock token and cube ingredient (US-019); cold weapon only with US-020.
- **Bemidji Blizzard**: Slow spell that also creates a temporary Fantasy pocket.

## Assumptions

- "Bemidji" is the intended name (Minnesota cold), not a placeholder.
- The can cozy (US-020) is not required to *cast* Blizzard; it is required to *wield* the can.

## Out of Scope

- Can cozy wielding (US-020).
- Cube recipe (US-019).
- Code Red / fireball (US-018).
- Fantasy occupancy and tile-drift rules themselves (US-003, US-004); this story only creates a pocket those rules consume.
