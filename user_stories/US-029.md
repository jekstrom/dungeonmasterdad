# US-029: Baja Blast Sugar Rush

**Status**: Draft  
**Source**: James — 3 Baja Blast specials  
**Priority**: P2  
**Roles**: Dungeon Master

## User Story

As the Dungeon Master fighting the Baja Blast boss, at half health the boss goes overcaffeinated for ten seconds: faster, more fragile, vibrating and blowing bubbles so the frenzy is obvious and then it ends.

## Context

Builds on US-017 boss HP. **Once** at 50% HP, a **timed 10s buff** — not a second phase that lasts until death. Move and attack speed up. Damage taken +15%. Boss vibrates; bubble particles emit. Host-authoritative. Distinct from Jet (US-027). US-028 is an environmental fountain slick, not a boss move. PP occupancy T011 unchanged.

## Independent Test

Damage the Baja Blast boss to 50% HP. Exactly once it enters Sugar Rush for ~10s: it moves and attacks faster, takes +15% damage, vibrates, emits bubbles. After 10s the buff ends and it is not stuck in a forever phase. A second window matches the buff window.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Given** the boss first crosses 50% HP, **When** the host resolves, **Then** Sugar Rush starts once.
2. **Given** Sugar Rush is active, **When** the boss moves and attacks, **Then** those speeds are increased for the duration.
3. **Given** Sugar Rush is active, **When** the boss takes a hit, **Then** damage taken is increased by 15%.
4. **Given** Sugar Rush is active, **When** a player looks, **Then** the boss vibrates and bubble particles emit.
5. **Given** 10s have elapsed, **When** the buff ends, **Then** speeds and damage taken return to baseline; it does not re-trigger at 50% again.

## Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: Sugar Rush MUST trigger **once** when HP first reaches **50%** (or drops below). It MUST NOT trigger again this match.
- **FR-002**: Duration MUST be **10s** (configurable). This is a timed buff, **not** a permanent second phase.
- **FR-003**: While active, move speed and attack speed MUST increase (configurable multipliers).
- **FR-004**: While active, damage taken MUST be **+15%**.
- **FR-005**: While active, the boss MUST vibrate and emit bubble particles. Particles may be local VFX; the buff itself is host-authoritative.
- **FR-006**: Buff start, remaining time, and end MUST replicate. Late join during the frenzy MUST see the buff.

## Multiplayer Requirements

- **MR-001**: Trigger, multipliers, and end MUST be server-authored.
- **MR-002**: Peers MUST agree whether Sugar Rush is active.

## Edge Cases

- Exact 50% from a hit that also kills: die wins; do not start a 10s frenzy on a corpse.
- Hit at 51% then 49% in one frame: still once.
- Jet mid-rush: allowed; rush does not replace Jet. The US-028 fountain keeps cycling on its own timer.
- Particles missing: buff still applies.

## Key Entities

- **Sugar Rush**: Once-per-match 10s overcaffeinated buff.

## Assumptions

- Suggested move/attack multipliers ~1.3–1.5× unless tuned in play.
- Implement after Jet (US-027); may ship with or after the US-028 fountain slick.

## Out of Scope

- Carbonated Jet (US-027) as a move. Fountain slick (US-028) as a doodad.
- US-019 cube, US-020 cozy, US-018 fireball.
- PP occupancy (US-003 T011).
- Bemidji Blizzard (US-017 T005).
