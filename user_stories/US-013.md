# US-013: Gremlins relocate resources

**Status**: Signed  
**Source**: `ideas.md` — monsters  
**Priority**: P2  
**Roles**: Dungeon Master, Paper Pusher

## User Story

As the Dungeon Master, I spawn gremlins that pick up and move resources around so that I can scatter the office's wood, iron, paper, and forms without having to destroy every factory.

## Context

Gremlin spawn is already a DM HUD action gated on Fantasy Level (mana in US-014). There is no dedicated gremlin **behavior** scene that carries items; world spawn must not keep using the goblin prefab. World pickups and inventories already exist.

This is harassment, not deletion: resources are moved, not destroyed.

**Hard art/identity constraint (James):** Gremlins are **distinct from goblins**. They MUST have **their own** sprite sheets, VFX, and UI/skill icons. Do **not** reuse `monsters/goblin.tscn` / `monsters/goblin.png`, recolor goblins, or share goblin attack/raid animations. Skill-tree gremlin row icons (US-034) and spawn HUD must read as gremlins, not goblins.

**Signed at `7d868d8` — story + tasks are the contract unless James overrides.**

**Locked behavior adds (James, still Signed):**

1. **Move speed** = **2×** the current baseline monster move speed used by the pre-fix gremlin/goblin placeholder (same multiplier on host for all peers).
2. **Pathing** stays on the **walkable** map only — no walking off cliffs, into void, or becoming stuck off-map / off-navmesh.
3. **Staples damage gremlins** — gremlins expose a hurtbox that US-005 staple projectiles can hit and damage (and kill) like other damageable monsters.
4. Gremlins **always flee Paper Pushers** — when a PP is in flee range, flee overrides pickup/carry targeting until the PP is out of range (still drop-on-death / carry rules apply).

## Independent Test

Drop wood (or iron/paper/forms) on the ground. Spawn a gremlin (gremlin visuals, not a goblin). It moves at clearly ~2× the old placeholder speed, pathfinds only on walkable cells to a resource, picks it up, carries it, and drops it somewhere else. A Paper Pusher approaches: the gremlin flees instead of continuing toward them. The PP shoots staples: the gremlin takes damage and can die (dropping any carried item). A Paper Pusher can still recover the pile. The gremlin does not delete the item or walk off the map.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Given** a world resource pickup exists (wood, iron, paper, blank form, filled form, tax form), **When** a gremlin has no carried item and is not fleeing, **Then** it pathfinds to one and picks it up.
2. **Given** a gremlin is carrying a resource, **When** it travels a configurable distance or time, **Then** it drops the resource as a world pickup at its current position.
3. **Given** a gremlin has dropped an item, **When** it is still alive and not fleeing, **Then** it may acquire another resource.
4. **Given** a Paper Pusher is in range of a dropped resource, **When** they pick it up, **Then** they receive it using the existing pickup/inventory rules.
5. **Given** a gremlin dies while carrying a resource, **When** death resolves, **Then** the resource drops at the gremlin's position and is not lost.
6. **Given** no world resources exist, **When** a gremlin wanders, **Then** it does not pull items out of player inventory by default.
7. **Given** a gremlin is inside the Reality Zone, **When** it relocates items, **Then** it is allowed (gremlins are not skeletons; US-001 does not ban them).
8. **Given** a spawned gremlin, **When** peers view it, **Then** it uses **gremlin-specific** world art/scene — not `monsters/goblin.tscn` and not a goblin recolor.
9. **Given** a living gremlin, **When** its move speed is compared to the pre-fix placeholder baseline, **Then** it moves at **2×** that baseline (host-authoritative).
10. **Given** a gremlin pathfinding (to a pile, while carrying, fleeing, or wandering), **When** it moves, **Then** every step stays on **walkable** navigation — it MUST NOT leave the walkable map, walk off cliffs, or become permanently stuck off-navmesh / off-map.
11. **Given** a Paper Pusher fires staples that hit a gremlin’s hurtbox, **When** damage resolves on the host, **Then** the gremlin loses HP and can die; death drops any carried resource (AC5).
12. **Given** a Paper Pusher within flee range of a gremlin, **When** the gremlin’s AI updates, **Then** it **always flees** that PP (pathing still walkable) and does not walk into the PP to pick up or harass; flee ends when no PP remains in flee range.

## Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: Gremlins MUST be able to pick up world resource pickups of the types listed in AC-1.
- **FR-002**: A gremlin MUST carry at most one stack/item instance at a time (US-039 Minions may raise this later).
- **FR-003**: Carry MUST end in a drop after a random delay in a configurable range (suggested 2s–6s) or when the gremlin is damaged, whichever the implementation uses consistently; death always drops.
- **FR-004**: Dropped items MUST remain valid pickups with the same item identity and quantity.
- **FR-005**: Gremlins MUST NOT destroy buildings (US-011) and MUST NOT deal lethal executes (US-012).
- **FR-006**: Pickup, carry, drop, damage, death, speed, and flee MUST be host-authoritative.
- **FR-007**: Gremlins SHOULD prefer resources near factories and the IRS when multiple piles exist (when not fleeing).
- **FR-008**: Gremlin world actor MUST be a dedicated scene (e.g. `monsters/gremlin.tscn`) driven by **gremlin** sprites — MUST NOT instance or atlas-reuse goblin sheets for the living actor.
- **FR-009**: DM spawn HUD / skill icons for gremlins MUST use gremlin-specific art (existing `sprites/gremlin_spawn_button.png` or successor), not goblin raid icons.
- **FR-010**: Gremlin move speed MUST be **2×** the current baseline monster move speed used by the pre-fix gremlin/goblin placeholder (tunable constant documented in code/comments).
- **FR-011**: Gremlin navigation MUST use walkable map / navmesh constraints only; path requests MUST NOT produce off-map, cliff, or permanently stuck positions.
- **FR-012**: Gremlins MUST expose a hurtbox compatible with US-005 staple projectiles so staples deal damage (and can kill).
- **FR-013**: While any Paper Pusher is within a configurable flee range, the gremlin MUST prioritize fleeing that PP over acquiring or pathing toward resources (carry may continue until drop timer / damage / death; do not walk into the PP).

## Multiplayer Requirements

- **MR-001**: All peers MUST see the same carried vs dropped state for each item.
- **MR-002**: Two gremlins MUST NOT pick up the same item instance.
- **MR-003**: All peers MUST see the same gremlin visuals (gremlin sheet, not goblin).
- **MR-004**: All peers MUST agree on gremlin HP / death and flee locomotion outcomes driven by the host.

## Edge Cases

- Player and gremlin grab the same pickup in one frame: server awards it to one actor; the other gets nothing.
- Drop inside Fantasy Zone: Paper Pushers cannot fetch it until it is moved out (US-003). That is allowed and is part of the harassment.
- Inventory-full player trying to reclaim: item stays on the ground (existing full-inventory behavior).
- US-039 / US-040 / US-041 passives: hook later; this story ships baseline carry-1 + visible gremlin.
- Flee while carrying: gremlin keeps the item until drop timer, damage policy, or death; flee does not delete the item.
- Multiple PPs: flee the nearest (or highest threat) PP; still walkable-only.
- Staples miss / non-staple damage: out of scope unless already shared monster damage pipeline applies.

## Key Entities

- **Gremlin**: Resource-relocating monster (distinct species from goblin); fast, frail-to-staples, flees PPs.
- **Carried Resource**: Item instance held by a gremlin, not in any player inventory.

## Assumptions

- Gremlins do not loot equipped weapons or staple magazines.
- Spawn cost/unlock stays on the DM HUD; payment becomes mana in US-014.
- `monsters/gremlin.png` may already exist as a sheet candidate; Art still validates frames (idle / walk / pick / carry) and that it is not a goblin atlas alias.
- “Current” speed baseline = whatever move speed the pre-fix placeholder gremlin summon used (typically the goblin actor speed). Flee range suggested ~ same order as PP attack range; tune in implementation.
- Staple damage numbers reuse existing US-005 / monster HP pipeline unless a gremlin-specific HP is set in the gremlin scene.

## Out of Scope

- Factory destruction / goblin raids (US-011).
- Knightling kills (US-012).
- Skill-tree passive effects for gremlins (US-039–041).
- Gremlins attacking or damaging Paper Pushers.
