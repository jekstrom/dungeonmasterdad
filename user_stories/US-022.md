# US-022: Pull-start chainsaw (unreliable endgame)

**Status**: Draft  
**Source**: `ideas.md` — Pull start  
**Priority**: P3  
**Roles**: Paper Pusher, Dungeon Master

## User Story

As a Paper Pusher in the endgame, I get a chainsaw that only sometimes starts when I pull the cord so that our last-stand weapon against Dad All Powerful is powerful, loud, and unreliable.

## Context

Ideas.md: "chainsaw", "end game", "it only sometimes works." This is the Paper Pusher counterpart to US-021. Default loadout remains staple gun + pen/pencil (US-005) until this weapon is available.

## Independent Test

Reach the endgame unlock. Equip/draw the chainsaw. Pull start several times: some pulls fail (no damage, pull-cord feedback), a successful pull starts the engine. While running, melee deals high damage to the DM and to monsters. The engine can stall, after which another successful pull is required. Before unlock, no player has a chainsaw.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Given** the chainsaw is locked, **When** a Paper Pusher uses weapon controls, **Then** they only have the US-005 loadout.
2. **Given** the endgame condition is met, **When** the server grants the chainsaw, **Then** at least one living Paper Pusher can draw it (default: all living Paper Pushers receive it).
3. **Given** the chainsaw is drawn and the engine is off, **When** the player uses primary fire / pull-start, **Then** the server rolls a start check; a failure deals no damage and spends no fuel.
4. **Given** a failed pull, **When** the player pulls again, **Then** they may succeed on a later roll (suggested: 35% base, +15% per consecutive failure, clamped at 90%).
5. **Given** a successful start, **When** the engine is running, **Then** melee swings deal high damage (suggested ≥3× pen/pencil) to the DM, monsters, and (optionally) nothing to friendly buildings.
6. **Given** the engine is running, **When** a stall check fails (suggested small chance per second, or on taking a hit), **Then** the engine stops and pull-start is required again.
7. **Given** the engine is running, **When** fuel reaches 0, **Then** the engine stops and cannot restart until refueled (suggested: Office Max does not refill fuel; fuel is a finite endgame tank).
8. **Given** another peer is watching, **When** pulls, starts, stalls, and hits happen, **Then** they see and resolve the same outcomes.
9. **Given** the player dies with a running engine, **When** they respawn, **Then** the engine is off and they still own the chainsaw if the grant was per-player.

## Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: The chainsaw MUST be locked until an endgame condition. Default condition: **Dad All Powerful has been unlocked** (US-021) **or** Reality Level has reached a high threshold (suggested 200), whichever comes first, so Paper Pushers are not helpless if the DM never cubes.
- **FR-002**: Starting the engine MUST be a discrete pull action with a server-side chance of failure ("it only sometimes works").
- **FR-003**: Failed pulls MUST NOT deal damage.
- **FR-004**: A running chainsaw MUST deal high melee damage and MUST consume fuel over time while running (even idle).
- **FR-005**: The engine MUST be able to stall randomly while running.
- **FR-006**: Staple gun and pen/pencil remain available; drawing the chainsaw replaces the active melee/primary until holstered.
- **FR-007**: Pulls, stalls, fuel, and hits MUST be host-authoritative.
- **FR-008**: The chainsaw MUST NOT start reliably 100% of the time even on the first pull of a fight.

## Multiplayer Requirements

- **MR-001**: Grant, pull rolls, and damage MUST run on the server.
- **MR-002**: All peers MUST agree whether each player's engine is running.

## Edge Cases

- Pull during form-fill channel: cancel the channel.
- Two Paper Pushers with chainsaws: independent engines and fuel.
- Pull-start success chance resets after a successful start, not after a stall only — after stall, consecutive-failure bonus may persist for the fight (suggested: persist until a successful start).
- Hitting a factory: default no friendly fire.

## Key Entities

- **Chainsaw**: Endgame Paper Pusher weapon.
- **Pull Start**: Unreliable engine start roll.
- **Fuel**: Finite runtime resource.

## Assumptions

- Humor and feel: lawn-equipment dad energy versus Dew-charged DM.
- Suggested fuel: 12s of run time per grant, not refillable in v1.
- "Sometimes works" is mandatory; a chainsaw that always starts fails this story.

## Out of Scope

- Staple gun (US-005).
- Dad All Powerful's own effects (US-021), except as an unlock trigger.
- Match victory screen.
