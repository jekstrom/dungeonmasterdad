# US-009: Forms, tax forms, and the IRS

**Status**: Draft  
**Source**: `ideas.md` — Paper Pushers, tax forms  
**Priority**: P2  
**Roles**: Paper Pusher

## User Story

As a Paper Pusher, I turn paper into forms, fill them out, and carry completed tax forms to the IRS building to file them so that paperwork — especially taxes — is the strongest way we raise Reality Level.

## Context

Paper comes from paper factories (US-006, US-008). Factory production already raises Reality; this story adds a second, stronger paperwork loop. Filing a tax form at the IRS must increase Reality Level **more than producing paper does**.

There is no form item and no IRS building yet.

## Independent Test

Produce paper, convert it to a blank form, fill it. A standard filled form grants some Reality. A filled tax form grants nothing until delivered to an IRS building, then grants a large Reality spike larger than one paper-factory cycle.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Given** a Paper Pusher has at least one paper, **When** they use the create-form action (or an Office/IRS interactable that consumes paper), **Then** one paper is consumed and one blank form is gained.
2. **Given** a Paper Pusher has a blank form, **When** they complete the fill-out action, **Then** the blank form becomes a filled form of the chosen type (standard or tax).
3. **Given** a filled standard form, **When** fill-out completes, **Then** Reality Level increases by the standard-form amount without requiring the IRS.
4. **Given** a filled tax form in inventory, **When** the player is not at the IRS, **Then** Reality Level does not increase from that form.
5. **Given** a Paper Pusher with a filled tax form is in range of an enabled IRS building, **When** they file, **Then** the tax form is consumed and Reality Level increases by the tax-file amount.
6. **Given** the tax-file amount, **When** compared to one successful paper-factory production cycle, **Then** the tax-file Reality gain is strictly larger.
7. **Given** no IRS building exists, **When** a player tries to file a tax form, **Then** filing fails and the form remains in inventory.
8. **Given** fill-out is interrupted (damage, death, move out of range if channeled), **When** the action ends, **Then** the blank form is not consumed into a filled form (progress may reset or pause; it must not double-complete).

## Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: Paper MUST convert 1:1 into a blank form via a server-validated action.
- **FR-002**: Blank forms MUST be fillable into either a **standard form** or a **tax form**.
- **FR-003**: Filling MUST take a configurable channel time (suggested default 3s standard, 6s tax) while the player stands still.
- **FR-004**: Completing a standard form MUST grant Reality Level immediately (suggested default +15).
- **FR-005**: Completing a tax form MUST NOT grant Reality Level until it is filed at an IRS building.
- **FR-006**: Filing a tax form at the IRS MUST consume the form and grant Reality Level strictly greater than paper-factory production (suggested default +50 vs paper factory +10).
- **FR-007**: The IRS MUST be a unique buildable (max one enabled IRS per match) placeable only under US-001 / US-003 rules, costing iron (US-007).
- **FR-008**: Form items MUST occupy inventory and can be dropped; gremlins (US-013) may move dropped forms.
- **FR-009**: All grants and consumptions MUST be host-authoritative.

## Multiplayer Requirements

- **MR-001**: Two players cannot file the same tax form instance.
- **MR-002**: Reality Level after filing MUST match on every peer.
- **MR-003**: IRS uniqueness MUST be enforced on the server (second placement rejected).

## Edge Cases

- Player dies while carrying a filled tax form: form drops as loot unless inventory persists through respawn (current respawn keeps inventory unless changed; do not silently delete the form).
- IRS destroyed by goblins (US-011) while a player is in the file interact: filing fails, form remains.
- Creating a form with 0 paper: rejected.

## Key Entities

- **Blank Form**: Paper product, unfilled.
- **Standard Form**: Filled in the field; modest Reality.
- **Tax Form**: Filled in the field; must be filed at IRS for large Reality.
- **IRS Building**: Unique drop-off for tax forms.

## Assumptions

- Form type is chosen when fill-out starts.
- Tax forms are the intended "big button" for Reality snowball, so they should be slower and require a field trip.

## Out of Scope

- Office Max (US-010) except that it is a different unique building.
- Factory Reality ticks (US-008).
