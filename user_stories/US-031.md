# US-031: Bemidji Blizzard spell

**Status**: Draft  
**Source**: `ideas.md` — Dungeon (Baja Blast boss → Bemidji Blizzard); split from US-017  
**Priority**: P2  
**Roles**: Dungeon Master, Paper Pusher

## User Story

As the Dungeon Master, I cast Bemidji Blizzard onto a rectangle of the office so that patch becomes a short Fantasy storm: Paper Pushers stay but move slower, factories tick slower, and new buildings cannot place — then the ice thaws.

## Context

US-017 is the **Baja Blast boss**: spawn at the dungeon exit, combat, death unlocks `bemidji_blizzard` and grants the can. This story is the **spell** that unlock enables.

ideas.md: Baja Blast boss unlocks **Bemidji Blizzard**, which **slows down factories and player movement**. Combined with US-003: the cast area is a temporary **Fantasy pocket**. Paper Pushers **walk it** (US-003 T011; no wall, no push-out). Occupancy and drift rules stay in US-003 / US-004; this story **creates** the pocket those rules consume and **adds** slow.

A first implementation already landed under US-017 T005–T008 (`DmManager.launch_blizzard`, factory `sync_blizzard_interval`, HUD button, ice overlay, late-join snapshot). This story owns that contract going forward: remaining gaps, tunables, and the independent test **without** requiring a boss fight (tests may `DmUnlocks.unlock("bemidji_blizzard")`).

## Independent Test

Unlock Bemidji Blizzard (US-017 kill, or a test unlock). With mana ≥ 30, target a rectangular patch of Reality home and confirm. Mana drops by 30. That rect is a Fantasy pocket for ~8s with **ice on the ground** and **falling snowflakes/icicles**. A Paper Pusher inside stays and moves at ~50% speed; outside they are baseline. A factory whose origin is in the rect takes ~2× as long to produce; a factory outside is unchanged. Buildings cannot place in the pocket. Existing buildings are not destroyed. When the duration ends, pocket, ice, speeds, and factory intervals return in the same tick. Locked or short mana: no pocket, no spend.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Given** Blizzard is locked, **When** the DM tries to cast it, **Then** nothing happens: no pocket, no slow, no mana spend, no overlay.
2. **Given** Blizzard is unlocked and mana is less than cost, **When** they confirm a target, **Then** the cast is rejected and mana is unchanged.
3. **Given** unlock + mana ≥ 30, **When** they complete a Bemidji Blizzard target, **Then** mana decreases by 30 (US-014 `try_cast("bemidji_blizzard")`) and one **axis-aligned Fantasy pocket** exists for ~8s.
4. **Given** a live blizzard pocket, **When** occupancy is evaluated, **Then** Paper Pushers may walk and remain (US-003 T011), new buildings cannot place, and skeletons may exist unless a Reality pocket covers the point.
5. **Given** a Paper Pusher **inside** the rect, **When** they move, **Then** their move speed is ~50% of baseline. **Leaving** the rect restores baseline even if the pocket is still live.
6. **Given** an enabled factory whose **origin** is in the pocket, **When** a production interval runs, **Then** that interval is ~2×; in-progress remaining time **scales**, it is not reset to 0. Factories outside the pocket are unchanged.
7. **Given** the duration ends, **When** the next occupancy / movement / production tick runs, **Then** the pocket is gone, ice overlay is gone, PP speeds and factory intervals are baseline, in the **same** tick.
8. **Given** a late-joining peer, **When** a blizzard is live, **Then** they receive unlock, pocket geometry + remaining duration, slow, factory timers, and ice.
9. **Given** goblins, knightlings, gremlins, the Baja Blast boss, or the DM, **When** they move in the pocket, **Then** this story does **not** slow them.
10. **Given** the DM has not exited the dungeon, **When** they cast, **Then** the spell still runs (not hard-gated on dungeon exit).

## Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: Cast MUST go through US-014 `try_cast("bemidji_blizzard")` (cost **30**, unlock id `bemidji_blizzard`). Reject locked or short mana with **no** world effect and **no** spend.
- **FR-002**: A successful cast MUST spend mana **atomically** with pocket creation. If the clipped rect is empty or `spawn_pocket` fails, mana MUST NOT be spent (today `launch_blizzard` can `try_cast` then fail `spawn_pocket` — that leak is this story).
- **FR-003**: Pocket MUST be an **axis-aligned rectangle**, optionally tile-snapped, using the fireball targeting confirm (`spells/targeting.tscn`), **not** a circle. Suggested size: **3×3** cells (`DungeonGrid.CELL_PX` 128). Clip to map interior / Fantasy `clip_pocket_rect` (US-024 / US-003).
- **FR-004**: Duration MUST be configurable (default **8s**). Overlay key `"blizzard"` so ice art is used, not grass.
- **FR-005**: Slow MUST apply to Paper Pusher movement **inside that rect** (factor **0.5**). It MUST NOT slow every PP on the map. Leaving the rect restores speed immediately.
- **FR-006**: Factory production whose **origin** lies in the live slow rect MUST use interval factor **2.0**. Remaining time scales on enter/leave; do not restart the tick. Destroyed / ghost factories MUST NOT tick (US-011).
- **FR-007**: Occupancy MUST follow US-003 T011 (walk, no wall, no push-out). Building placement intersecting the live Fantasy pocket MUST reject (US-003 T007). Existing buildings MUST NOT be destroyed by occupancy.
- **FR-008**: Expire MUST drop the pocket, slow list, ice, and factory factors in the same tick (FR-008 from US-017).
- **FR-009**: Effects MUST be host-authoritative. Late joiners MUST get current unlock + live pocket + slows + factory remaining.
- **FR-010**: Two casts in one frame: process sequentially; the second fails if mana is now short (US-014 edge). Multiple live pockets MAY coexist; each factory uses the slow if **its origin** is in **any** live blizzard rect.
- **FR-011**: Unlock and can grant remain US-017. Tests MAY unlock without a boss. Casting MUST NOT require the can cozy (US-020) or cube (US-019).

## Multiplayer Requirements

- **MR-001**: Validation, spend, pocket spawn, expire, slow, and factory timers MUST run on the host.
- **MR-002**: All Paper Pusher clients in the same rect MUST use the same speed factor.
- **MR-003**: Clients MUST NOT create a pocket by faking HUD confirm.

## Edge Cases

- Factory interval 90% complete when slow starts: remaining time scales; do not reset to 0.
- Player respawns during blizzard: Reality spawn (US-001). If they land in the pocket they stay and are slowed (T011). Do not shove them out.
- Blizzard over a factory: occupancy does not destroy it; it keeps producing at the slowed rate until expire (or US-011 goblin raids destroy it independently).
- Newer Reality pocket overlapping the blizzard: newer pocket wins for **claim**. Default: blizzard **slow** lasts the spell duration on the **cast rect** even if a Reality pocket later covers it.
- Clip to zero cells (off-map target): reject, no spend.
- `spawn_pocket` returns −1 after a successful `try_cast`: must not happen; spend only after a live pocket id.

## Key Entities

- **Bemidji Blizzard**: Mana-paid DM spell; Fantasy pocket + slow.
- **Blizzard slow rect**: Host list on `DmManager` (`_blizzard_effects`), keyed to a Fantasy pocket id.
- **Ice overlay**: `sprites/blizzard_overlay.png` on pocket cells while live.

## Assumptions

- "Bemidji" is the intended name (Minnesota cold).
- Can cozy (US-020) is not required to **cast**.
- Unlock bit is `bemidji_blizzard` (US-017 / `DmUnlocks`).
- Defaults: cost 30, duration 8s, PP factor 0.5, factory interval 2×, pocket 3×3 cells.

## Out of Scope

- Baja Blast **boss**, death unlock, and can grant (US-017).
- Can cozy wielding (US-020).
- Cube recipe (US-019).
- Code Red / fireball (US-018).
- Occupancy and drift **rules** (US-003 including T011, US-004); this story calls the pocket contract.
- Game over when one zone covers the map.
- Slowing monsters or the DM.
